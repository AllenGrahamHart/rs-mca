# Exact Controls And The Four-Pencil Guard

Over F97 take G=X*(X-1)*(X-2), T=G*span(1,X,X^2),
V=T+span(1) and

    W=(T,0)+span((1,G),(0,1)).

It is regular at every finite coordinate. Its generic kernel has second
component proportional to 1-z*G, which never vanishes identically in z
at a coordinate. The maximal constant pencil has dimension3.
At the three roots of G the child equals (T,0); elsewhere it has rank2
and a constant plane. D5,E2 gives the exact exceptional count3.

Replacing G by X^2*(X-1) gives two distinct exceptional points, not three.
Taking G=1, complement X^5 and the analogous pairs gives no exceptional
finite coordinate despite positive D-E slack at infinity.
These controls test all97 anchors, constant directions and actual ranks.

The four-pencil guard is essential. With V=span(1,X,X^2,X^3),

    W=(V,0)+span((0,1))

is regular at every coordinate and EVERY child is whole constant3.
One must not choose an arbitrary three-plane inside its four-pencil and
apply the theorem's exceptional-root claim.

These are algebraic controls, not official large-agreement MCA sources.
