    v=J-8-D-z>=0,
    N=R+D+1+v, A=d0+D+1-t+v, R=1048576,d0=67472.

The new algebraic supplier charges at most D-E such coordinates and yields

    Omega_t <= ((R+1+v)*C_t+(D-E)*max(U_t(E)-C_t,0))

    (R+1)*(L_t-C_t) > (K-E0)*max(U_t(E1)-C_t,0)

Hence the numerator is at most (R+1)*L_t+v*C_t.
Since C_t<=L_t and d0+1-t<=R+1,

    ((R+1)*L_t+v*C_t)/(d0+1-t+v)
       <= (R+1)*L_t/(d0+1-t).

This proves the original penultimate allowance for ALL slack and
normalization. No last-anchor centre, original raw weight, full gcd
or higher-resource term has been dropped.
