# Whole-Constant Child Cost Depends On Its Primitive Degree

Let E be the primitive scalar degree of the fixed maximal constant
three-pencil. At an exceptional regular eighth anchor, its actual
affine child has scalar dimension at most3. Let C be its complete core
union and e=R+J-|C|. The original constant-pencil source gate gives

    g+1<=e<=R-d0+t.

All eight old anchors are in the common complete cores. Remove them,
subtract an actual pair and divide scalar differences by their locator.
The scalar list parameters are initially

    length=R+J-e-8, degree bound=J-8, agreement=d0+J-t-8.

Let G be the remaining full scalar gcd and z its distinct roots on
C minus the anchors. Delete those z coordinates and divide by G.
The receiver is divided pointwise on the surviving domain; it need
not be a divisible polynomial. The primitive degree bound is E+1.
Unused degree and non-domain/repeated gcd roots give

    v=J-8-(E+1)-z>=0,
    length=R-e+v+E+1,
    agreement>=d0-t+v+E+1.

Delete ANY v further coordinates. Each scalar word loses at most v
agreements. The remaining length is at least E+1, so restriction remains
injective on degree-at-most-E polynomials. This gives an ordinary scalar
LIST on (r,w,K,s)=(R-e,d0-t,E+1,3).

On a complement box[a,b] and degree band[E0,E1], padding the length to
R-a+E+1 and using the uniform-in-degree theorem gives the exact cap
C3(a,E1+1) at

    (r,w,Kmax,s)=(R-a,d0-t,E1+1,3).

All padding is in the same original field. There is only ONE preferred
original finite slope for the whole constant pencil, charged at most t;
outside its union the original defects owned by a pair are disjoint.
Thus an exceptional child's total ORIGINAL raw weight is at most

    t+e*list_count <= t+b*C3(a,E1+1).

For a vertical direction with no preferred finite slope, t is conservative.
The finite maximum U_t(E1) over complement boxes is a uniform upper bound
for every E in the band. No monotonicity of the numerical selector's
output is assumed: every proposed trace proves the full degree interval.
