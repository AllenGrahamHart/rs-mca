# Two Primitive-Degree Bands Suffice

Use the SAME13 original degree profiles and both raw cutoffs.
Write K=J1-8. The primitive scalar degree E is covered by

    [2,min(11999,K)] and, if K>=12000, [12000,K].

Each is a whole interval, not a sampled endpoint. Every complement
e in[g+1,R-d0+t] is covered by consecutive width10000 boxes.
Each proposed scalar dimension3 LIST trace is checked against the
proved Johnson/padding/dimension inequalities.

For a band[E0,E1] define

    U=max_boxes(t+b*C3(a,E1+1)),
    extra=(K-E0)*max(U-C_t,0),
    gap=(R+1)*(L_t-C_t)-extra.

Every gap is positive. The resulting26 profile/cutoff comparisons fit
the original eighth-anchor allowance. No new allowance is selected.

The degree-sensitive factor matters. Using K instead of K-E makes the
coarse recipe fail in the final two profiles. Those failures are upper-
bound recipe limitations, not official counterexamples or necessary costs.
Whole-constant children can exceed L_t individually and still be paid
in this aggregate incidence argument.
