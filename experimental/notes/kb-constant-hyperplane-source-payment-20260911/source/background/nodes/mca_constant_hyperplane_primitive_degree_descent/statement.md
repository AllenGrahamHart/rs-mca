# Primitive-Degree Descent For A Constant Hyperplane

Use one original weighted MCA source with parameters
(n,K,core)=(R+J,J,d+J-t), and a regular prefix with shared dimension s
and pair dimension2s-3. All polynomial differences have undergone the
same original anchors and full-gcd changes of coordinates.
Assume R>=d>t; every price construction must also satisfy its printed
same-field padding and integer-mass conditions.
Write its primitive shared degree as D and unused degree as v>=0:

    N=R+D+1+v, A=d+D+1-t+v.

Suppose its pair enclosure contains a constant-direction (s-1)-dimensional
subspace S, but no constant s-dimensional subspace. Let E be the primitive
scalar degree of S; E>=s-2. For s>=4, every regular anchor is of one type:

1. Away from the common roots of S, the child again has a maximal
   constant hyperplane, and its primitive scalar degree is <=E-1.
2. At a common root, the child has a FULL constant (s-1)-dimensional
   subspace; its primitive shared degree is the SAME E.

There are at most D-E root-exceptional coordinates. A full constant
subspace, once present, persists under every regular anchor and loses
at least one primitive degree per step.

The exact original-weight recurrence in normalized_recurrence.md follows.
It uses degree-refined actual constant-pencil prices, not assumed
occupation of any formal subspace. No normalization cap or separability
hypothesis is needed. All cost inputs must be proved nonnegative upper
bounds; the finite consumer supplies them.
