# Hand Audit

Empty universal carrier core does NOT mean every source evaluation is
nonzero. It does mean every joint-core evaluation is nonzero, because
the allowed polynomial pair at a carrier zero has forced values(h_*,0).
The chosen m-T subset therefore satisfies the exact basis theorem inputs.

The r_gamma insertion counts are disjoint: each tuple has exactly one
coordinate outside the FIXED chosen subset H. We do not add tuple counts
from several core subsets or from several minimizing polynomials.

The all-raw baseline has g=0 and covers raw>T, including raw>d. HIGH
is needed even when the LOW basis bound is strong. The floor in MASS
is taken after division; the weighted sum is an integer. TAIL uses the
minimum weight ONE on low labels, not a fictitious weight t on all of them.

Small actual polynomial sources check the recoverable count with multiple
defects. A tiny exhaustive integer ledger checks every TAIL and rejects
missing multiplicity. These controls do not prove the universal suppliers.

OWNER counts selected defects outside the COMPLETE joint core, not outside
the chosen basis subset. The same-owner coordinates cannot be reused by
different slopes, while different owners may reuse them. Integer owner
controls retain this distinction; the theorem provides no free pair census.
