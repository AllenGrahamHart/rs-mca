two independent coefficient functionals. Require the three(CHAIN)
identities for each chosen pair, not just that one selected parameter
point lies on a quadric. Joint-core evaluations of V must be nonzero.

All defects and labels are original. At most K-s+2 labels are excepted
when their moving carrier vanishes on a core point. The tuple resource
is common to all labels; its multiplicity is TWO. No per-label core
cancellation or per-carrier source budget may be summed. Higher raw
labels and original near require separate, source-bound funding.
