# Contract

One fixed field/domain, received pair, affine pair offsets and carrier U.
Distinct original finite labels; exact selected scalar agreements; actual
chosen-pair defect multiplicities1..T. No minimality or auxiliary error
rank hypothesis. Require s>=1 and d>=T>=1.

Only the auxiliary universal zero core is cancelled. Original supports
may omit its coordinates; chosen core subsets and all original defects
survive. Offsets need not belong to U. Output is sum of original defects,
not number of pairs, a full-source payment or a Prize theorem.
