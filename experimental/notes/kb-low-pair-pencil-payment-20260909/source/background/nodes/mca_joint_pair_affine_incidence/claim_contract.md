# Claim Contract

- Object: distinct degree-<K polynomial pairs agreeing jointly on a fixed
  domain, not slopes, supports, graph points or separate scalar lists.
- Base affine dimension r is over F. The determinant condition is rank
  two over F(X); these are different invariants.
- The rank-two anchor loses two affine dimensions only at its first
  section. No rank-two assertion is made for subsequent sections.
- Every counted pair retains at least A agreements on the original domain.
  Bad anchors cost e in both incidence counts; A-e must be positive.
- The coarse determinant corollary requires A>2K-2. A smaller actual
  bad set may instead be used directly in (TWO).
- The affine special case has no characteristic restriction. Conic-specific
  hypotheses of the credited supplier are not used.
- No source normalization or pair-to-slope conversion is supplied here.
