# Result

PROVED: a jointly agreeing polynomial-pair family of affine dimension r
admits a rank-two first-anchor count whenever its direction space has
function-field rank two. With A>2K-2, the pair cap is

    (n-2K+2)/(A-2K+2) * ((n-K+1)/(A-K+1))^(r-2).

The rational-pencil exception and actual bad-anchor costs are necessary;
both have explicit finite controls. This lemma itself counts no MCA slopes.
