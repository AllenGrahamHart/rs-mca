# Focused Audit

An internal tuple is either dependent or has the known compatible parameter
as its unique solution. Consequently the entire set of internal tuples is
unavailable after the finite kernel label is charged. Removing only a
putative independent count, or puncturing U in the code, is not the proof.

The F7 finite-chart example has one genuine full-code-bad record whose
six independent tuples are all internal: omitting its kernel-label charge
would be false. The infinite-chart control has only dependent internal
tuples. Two actual full-code-bad records under u+X*v=0 show why a variable
rational direction does not justify the one-slope subtraction.

These are small interface controls, not original Prize-row counterexamples.
The exact recordwise lower counts and slope/near composition are explicitly
left to the consumer. Independent mathematical review remains due.
