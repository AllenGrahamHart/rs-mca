# Claim Contract

Object: independent ordered bases of actual polynomial evaluation spaces.
Quantifier: every nonzero-evaluation rank-r space and every finite smooth
branch family satisfying the printed lower-bound and real-interval gates.
Output: the minimum of the spike and equal-fiber endpoint expressions.

Only the minimum of the input branches must bound actual children.
Individual branches need not do so. The envelope need not be convex or
smooth. Every branch separately obeys SHAPE; real relaxation never asserts
the existence of a fractional-degree code. This is not a source census.
No received line, field, raw value, source rank or label is changed.

Required proof: exact full-fiber contraction and its rank-three seed.
The finite fixtures are controls, not the universal proof or a Prize bound.
