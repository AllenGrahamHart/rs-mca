# Audit

PROVED by the universal contraction and original-owner proofs plus bounded
exact rational certificates. Independent external review remains due.

Primary verify.py uses shifted-degree polynomials and the generic node's
polynomial helper. verify_audit.py imports neither and reconstructs the
tree in the original degree variable using explicit binomial substitution.
Both retain all256 endpoint branches for each of three core cutoffs.

They check765 input-branch SHAPE gates over entire real intervals,
512 coefficientwise intermediate transfers, both interval monotonicities,
and the completed HIGH151 gate. The independent audit also checks exact
floors at9941,9965,14000,21499 and rejects24 adjacent wrong floors.
No numerical J scan, floating-point bound or carrier sampling is used.

The finite certificates are the printed fixed recurrence and its exact
nonnegative polynomial controls, reconstructed by both scripts. They
prove only those finite gates; the generic concavity argument and original
tuple ownership still require hand proof. A minimum of branch bounds is
used consistently; no individual branch is asserted as a lower bound.

T150 is an intermediate core tool, not a new all-record truncation.
All raw ranges and near accounting are explicit. No global software audit,
whole-degree movement or Prize closure is claimed.
