# Complete Colour Classes And A Restricted Universe

By maximum-margin complete-core selection, every canonical record with
raw r<=T has a unique minimizing b on its COMPLETE scalar agreement A,
and A=H disjoint_union D_r with |D_r|=r. The selected maximizing support
contains every point of D_r. The guard2T<d is the one required by that
supplier.

Write h=h_*+w, w in V. On a fixed nonzero evaluation fibre,
h-h_*=lambda_x*ell(w) and b=lambda_x*ell(b). If one point of a
receiver-colour class(alpha,beta) belongs to A, then

    alpha+gamma*beta=ell(w),

so EVERY point of that colour class belongs to A. If one is a defect,
then beta!=ell(b), so EVERY point of the class is a defect.
Its cardinality is therefore at most |D_r|=r. This proves the bank
inclusion simultaneously for every t>=r.

Changing ell by a nonzero scalar scales both receiver-colour entries
uniformly and leaves their equality classes unchanged. Changing the
affine representative h_* within h_*+V translates the first colour
uniformly on the fibre, again preserving class sizes. Equivalently,
these are the projective fibres of the augmented evaluation vector
with nonzero V part. No assumption of constant receiver values on a
whole evaluation fibre is made.

At a zero of every polynomial in V, all allowed h equal h_*. If v!=0,
only gamma=-(u-h_*)/v can agree, regardless of its explanation or owner.
Removing the set of these labels costs at most the number z of those
coordinates, not z per pair. If v=0, all b in V equal v there, so the
coordinate is never a defect. Thus every defect of a remaining low
record lies in B_r. The universal-core hypothesis needed by a later
basis resource is separate from this bank statement.

The locator of the z zero-evaluation points divides every polynomial
in V. Its multiples of degree<K form a space of dimension K-z, so
s<=K-z and z<=K-s. No receiver-colour class is counted as a polynomial
zero-evaluation fibre.

Now take the tuple families in the statement. Each tuple contains one
actual defect outside its owner's joint core, hence contains a coordinate
of B_t. Other core entries MAY also be in B_t. The total number of
ordered distinct(s+1)-tuples touching a bank of size sigma_t is exactly

    n_falling_(s+1)-(n-sigma_t)_falling_(s+1).

Disjointness between different original labels and the recordwise lower
cost r*beta give the asserted weighted inequality. This restricts a
single existing tuple universe; it does not add another resource or
assume independently chosen tuple banks are disjoint.

Finally, outside the complete core of one fixed pair, a defect has v-b!=0
and (u-a)+gamma*(v-b)=0 fixes at most one gamma. Its different labels
therefore have disjoint defect sets inside B_t. Their total raw weight
is at most sigma_t.
