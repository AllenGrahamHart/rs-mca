# Rational Compression Fibres Share One Degree-Corrected Budget

Status: PROVED locally; independent mathematical review remains due.

Use a subfamily of one original finite-label MCA assignment, with original
pair degrees<K, complete joint cores H_f of size>=A, and fixed owner
weights omega_t(f)=sum of original raw values for assigned raw<=t labels.
All cores contain a distinct anchor set of size a<K with locator H_a.

Suppose the divided pair-direction enclosure W has a codimension-one
function-field-rank-one subspace
H={(A1*u,-A0*u):u in T}, primitive coprime row (A0,A1) of height h,
and W itself has function-field rank two. Let f_c range over ACTUAL
fibres of the quotient W/H and C_c be each fibre's complete-core union.
If A>K+h-1, define S_h=n-K-h+1 and x_c=|C_c|-K-h+1. Then

    x_c>=A-K-h+1>0, sum_c x_c<=S_h.                    (PACK)

A scalar LIST cap L(x) applies on the SAME-field auxiliary parameters

    length=x+K+h-1-a, degree=K-a-h, agreement=A-a,

and dimension at most dim T, or its actual represented scalar span.
If h>0, the total ORIGINAL raw weight is bounded by

    |union_c C_c|+sum_c(S_h-x_c)*L(x_c)
      <=n+sum_c(S_h-x_c)*L(x_c).                       (WEIGHT)

For h=0 replace the first term by t. The first term is global, not per
fibre. No receiver or base pair is divided, and no owner weight is reset.
Unlike the earlier constant-compression statement, the complementary
pair need not have its components in the scalar parameter space T.

If dim W=dim U=3, W subset U x U, and dim H=2, then for h>0

    T=g*span(A0,A1), U=g*span(A0^2,A0*A1,A1^2),
    2h<=K-a-1.                                       (NORMAL)

For h=0 only dim T=2 is needed. A whole rank-one W is NOT covered:
the nonzero quotient annihilator needed for PACK would then be absent.
No universal terminal cover, original-source payment or Prize closure
is asserted by this generic theorem.
