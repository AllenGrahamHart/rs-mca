- Resource: one positive shifted-core budget n-K-h+1.
- Preferred/inside charge: one global union size for h>0, one t for h=0.
- Scalar receiver: defined pointwise after dividing differences only.
- Specialization: dim W=dim U=3 and dim H=2 force the nonconstant
  quadratic-product carrier, and hence 2h<=K-a-1.
- Excluded: whole rank-one W; no nonzero quotient annihilator then exists.
- Nonclaims: no complete terminal cover or original bad-slope payment.
