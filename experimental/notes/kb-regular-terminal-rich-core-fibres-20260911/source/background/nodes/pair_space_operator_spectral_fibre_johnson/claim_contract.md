# Claim Contract

- Object: actual polynomial pairs and their complete joint cores.
- Field: original F for both eigenvalues and projective evaluations.
- Operator: dimension3; every F-eigenspace has dimension<=1.
- Root mask: anchors plus F-eigenpolynomial roots; common zeros if e=0.
- Fibre bound: maximum ACTUAL fibre on the remaining finite domain.
- Gate: A-b_e>0 and (A-b_e)^2>(n-b_e)*H.
- Output: integer pair count, then original weight via n-A.
- Not claimed: generic-degree substitution, full terminal coverage,
  a bound with multidimensional eigenspaces, or a Prize resolution.

If an F-eigenspace has dimension2, its graph is a constant-direction
rank-one plane. A pencil-free operator terminal therefore satisfies the
eigenspace hypothesis. A whole scalar operator does not.
