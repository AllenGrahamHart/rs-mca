labels, full-code badness, shared core and original rank remain fixed.

its affine pair dimension exceeds eleven; no product of scalar lists is used.

not to a possibly lower-dimensional subset of counted pairs. Its carrier
has common zeros, which the generic theorem explicitly allows.

original P_2 core union. No rank-two hypothesis is imposed on children.

rational line chosen through only one pair need not. Singleton intersections
are handled separately in the hereditary argument.
