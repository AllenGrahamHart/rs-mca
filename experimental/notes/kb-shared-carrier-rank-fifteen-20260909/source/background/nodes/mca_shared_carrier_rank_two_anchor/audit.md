# Audit

The proof controls the span of bad evaluation functionals, not the span
of arbitrary rank-one tensors. Independence is justified by projecting a
tensor relation onto both copies of the already independent ell_i.
Zero evaluation functionals are retained in the root set. The annihilator
has positive dimension only because r>s.

Good sections use the actual common V_x; separate component dimensions
cannot be substituted. Their affine dimension drops by two while scalar
carrier dimension drops by one. Constant-pencil rigidity holds even after
such a section acquires common zeros.

verify.py checks all156 hyperplanes in F5^4 for each of V=<1,X> and
V=<X,X^2>, plus sharp root sets and the failing r=s shortcut. These
small controls are not the proof of the arbitrary-field statement.
Independent external mathematical review remains due.
