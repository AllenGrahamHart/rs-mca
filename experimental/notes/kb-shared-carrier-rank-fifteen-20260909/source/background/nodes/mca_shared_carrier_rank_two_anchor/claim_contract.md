- Object: joint polynomial pairs in ONE shared scalar carrier V.
- Field: arbitrary F; all ranks and dimensions in (BAD) are over F.
- Scope: s<r<=2s for the improved anchor; r=s only for pencil rigidity.
- Required gate: A>K+s-r. The bad set includes common carrier zeros.
- Output: bad-coordinate bound, two-constraint section and pair incidence.
- Not supplied: a census of all sources, a repeated-rank-drop hypothesis,
  or an MCA label charge.
- Falsifier: a valid W with more than K+s-r bad coordinates, or a
  nonconstant rank-s pencil inside V x V with dim V=s.
