# Off-Pencil Pairs Escape A Compatible Core Union

Status: PROVED. This is a joint polynomial-pair LIST statement; its MCA
projection and original-source accounting belong to the consumer.

Let D contain n distinct field elements, and let (u,v) be arbitrary received
values on D. Polynomial pairs have component degrees <K. Let a rational
pencil have primitive coprime row

    A0*a+A1*b=Q,  h=max(deg A0,deg A1),  deg Q<K+h.

Let U be any subset of D on which A0*u+A1*v=Q, and e=n-|U|. In
particular, U may be the union of COMPLETE joint agreement cores of any
represented pairs on that pencil; compatibility holds even at gcd roots.

For an OFF-pencil pair with at least m-T joint agreements on D, put

    A=m-T-(K+h-1).

If A>0, it has at least A joint agreements on D\U. If A>e, no such
pair exists. If 1<=A<=e and A^2>e*(K-1), their number M satisfies

    M <= floor(e*(A-K+1)/(A^2-e*(K-1))).             (ESC)

Every distinct polynomial pair is counted once. No scalar-list product,
field change, or deletion of original MCA records is involved. This is an
auxiliary restriction for counting pairs, not a smaller original source.
The positive denominator is a sufficient gate, not a necessary condition
for small lists or for a safe MCA row.

More generally, if both components lie in affine translates of the SAME
dimension-at-most-s polynomial space, ANY proved shared-carrier joint-LIST
cap on (e,K,A) bounds M. This includes the required padded dimension
compiler even when the direct Johnson denominator is nonpositive. Enlarging
the auxiliary domain with arbitrary received values, enlarging K, or lowering
A gives a valid upper bound whenever that supplier's gates hold. None of
these auxiliary relaxations changes the original MCA source.

The row-height h is essential. A moving pencil can meet an off-pencil pair
in K+h-1 coordinates; replacing that by K-1 is generally false.

For a nonempty pencil group of bounded-degree pairs, its row Q has the
required degree by evaluating it on one pair. If at least two distinct
bounded-degree pairs lie on the pencil, its primitive height satisfies h<K.
These facts use the credited rational-pencil normalization proof.
