# Pencil Section Caps Propagate Through Joint Affine Incidence

Status: PROVED. Let S be a finite family of degree-<K polynomial pairs,
each having at least A joint agreements with one receiver on n distinct
coordinates, where 2K-2<A<=n. Put

    H=(n-2K+2)/(A-2K+2).

Suppose P>=1, H<=P^2, and the following scoped section bound holds:
for EVERY affine F-subspace Z of degree-<K pairs whose direction space
has rank at most one over F(X),

    |S intersect Z| <= P^(dim_F Z).                   (PC)

Then for EVERY affine F-subspace Z of pair dimension r,

    |S intersect Z| <= P^r.                          (AFF)

If its direction space has rank TWO over F(X), the sharper bound is

    |S intersect Z| <= H*P^(r-2).                    (TWO)

S need not span Z. Children of a rank-two anchor may be pencils; (PC)
handles them explicitly. The premise controls intersections with ALL affine
pencil subspaces, not only the total count on each full rational line.

This is a proved implication with an exact section hypothesis. Its finite
consumer derives that hypothesis from an already PROVED whole-source
payment by contradiction; no speculative census premise is installed.
