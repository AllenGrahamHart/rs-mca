# Claim Contract

Object: a geometrically integral rational nondegenerate curve of degree d
in P^r, r>=3; characteristic0 or p>d. Inner-map degrees include
inseparability; the low-degree guard proves the separability needed here.

Output: generic smooth inner birationality and the finite total charge
sum binom(b_P+mu_P-1,2)<=binom(d-r+1,2). Centre count and branch count
are different quantities. Original-coordinate conversion uses nu times
the branch count, with the stated integer-ratio refinement.

Auxiliary geometric centres prove a count only. They are not legal
original anchors by assertion, nor is the resulting bound guaranteed
to fit any original incidence budget.
