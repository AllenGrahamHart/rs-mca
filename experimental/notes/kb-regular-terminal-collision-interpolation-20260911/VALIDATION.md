## What The Checks Do Not Prove

Hashes certify byte identity, not truth. Local PROVED statuses and finite
controls are not external proof review. Universal actual-source ownership,
spectral geometry, the principal-kernel argument, and multiplicity pullback
must be hand reviewed at their full printed scopes.

The replay verifies exact certificate inequalities and the unchanged parent
weight template. It does not enumerate official sources, prove low-image
payment, decide either Prize problem, or establish an active-v4 atom.
Unrelated inherited arithmetic, the global software/crosswalk suite and
canonical Fable work were not replayed in this publication.

No compute request accompanies this packet. The next useful work is
mathematical control of actual low-image collision groups and the whole
constant-direction upper tail, not a large numerical census.
