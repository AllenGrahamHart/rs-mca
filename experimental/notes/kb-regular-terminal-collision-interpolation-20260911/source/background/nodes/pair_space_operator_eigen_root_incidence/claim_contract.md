# Claim Contract

- Object: actual pairs in one affine operator graph, not all hull points.
- Domain: no common evaluation zero of U; common zeros must be removed first.
- Field: eigenvalues and eigenspaces over the original F.
- Capacities: q1 for all affine eigen-direction lines, q2 for all affine
  planes, with 2*q1<=q2; hereditary under selecting a smaller family.
- Count: core INCIDENCES at eigen roots, not their number times all pairs.
- Energy: one fixed M-element subset; integer/exact-rational arithmetic.
- Excluded: a two-dimensional F-eigenspace or unproved line/plane capacity.
- Nonclaims: an unrestricted row bound, reset original raw weights,
  generic-degree substitution, or a Prize resolution.
