# Product Rank Is The Hilbert Function Of The Actual Plane Image

Let d0 be the actual maximum degree in U and homogenize a basis to binary
forms ui(X,Z) of degree d0. The homogeneous substitution
F[Y0,Y1,Y2]->F[X,Z] has prime kernel I. Two basis elements have a
nonconstant ratio r in F(X/Z), since they are F-linearly independent.
In its fraction field, r and Z^d0*u0(X/Z) are algebraically independent:
the first is transcendental over F and the second is transcendental over
F(X/Z). The image algebra therefore has transcendence degree two.

The polynomial-ring dimension formula gives height(I)=1. Since a polynomial
ring over a field is a UFD, I is generated by one irreducible homogeneous
polynomial F0. Write eta=deg F0. Linear independence of the basis gives
eta>=2. These are polynomial identities, not equations inferred from a
finite sample of evaluation points. The standard height-one fact is also
recorded in [Stacks, Lemma 10.120.6](https://stacks.math.columbia.edu/tag/0AFT).

In degree ell, multiplication by F0 is injective, and its image is exactly
the kernel of substitution. Subtracting dimensions of homogeneous forms
therefore gives

    H_U(ell)=binom(ell+2,2)-binom(ell-eta+2,2),

with the latter term zero for ell<eta. Dehomogenization is injective on
binary forms of the fixed degree d0*ell, so this is also the rank of
the original univariate product space. No asymptotic Hilbert-polynomial
approximation is used. For fixed ell the rank is nondecreasing in eta.
The plane-curve principal-equation sequence is also stated in
[Stacks, Section 53.9](https://stacks.math.columbia.edu/tag/0BYA).

For example U=span(1,X^s,X^(eta*s)) has image equation
Y0^(eta-1)*Y2-Y1^eta=0, independently of the compositional degree s.
Its polynomial degree eta*s and possibly repeated finite fibres must not
be confused with eta. The incidence proof counts their pullback multiplicity
directly and never substitutes generic fibre size for finite maximum size.
