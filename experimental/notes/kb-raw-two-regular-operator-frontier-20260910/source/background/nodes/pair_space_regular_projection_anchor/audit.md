# Audit

A joint-good coordinate need not preserve generic projection. Over F101,
V=span(1,X,X^2) and W=span((1,0),(X,1),(X^2,0),(0,X^2)) have full
generic projection. Joint evaluation at0 has rank two, but its section
projects only onto span(X^2), not the two-dimensional V_0. The generic
kernel's second component is span(X^2), so the repaired set excludes0.

The proof uses the constant part of an F(z)-annihilator; its F-dimension
bound follows from scalar-extension independence, not a count of sampled
normals. The good-section lifting argument uses a generic kernel vector
only to prove rank, and constant matrices then descend the rank to F.
Actual pair families may have smaller span than the displayed enclosure.

The bounded fixture verifies the failure and repair at every F101 point.
It also retains a non-diagonalizable operator terminal. It is NOT a
full-code-bad MCA source, source counterexample or universal proof by scan.
