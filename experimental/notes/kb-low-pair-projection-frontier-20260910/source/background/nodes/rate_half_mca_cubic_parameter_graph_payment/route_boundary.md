# Raising The Degree Alone Cannot Pay The Next Source Classes

This is a boundary of the PRINTED sufficient recipe, not an impossibility
theorem for higher-degree incidence or for the original Prize bound.

Keep the same original W0, uniform J9965 endpoint, moving-kernel
dimension10 and deficit conversion. For degree4<=e<=10 and1<=T<=43,
the generic lemma would yield the raw-mass cap

    M_(e,T)=floor[e*(R+9965)_11 /
                   (11*(d+9965-T)*product_(j=1)^9(d-T+j))].

It is nondecreasing in both e and T. Its smallest value in this range is

    M_(4,1)=302790601542847445 > B*-134944.

Also W0=624373932788019251 is larger than M_(4,1). The same sufficient
whole-source envelope, even before nonnegative exceptions are included,
is bounded BELOW by

    floor[(W0+T*M_(e,T))/(T+1)]+134944
        >=M_(4,1)+134944=302790601542982389>B*.

Thus simply increasing the moving-normal degree cannot extend these
uniform payments beyond the cubic using this basis count and original
ledger. New basis mass, ownership information, a stronger original
resource or an exhaustive different structure would be needed. This
does not rule out higher-degree source-class bounds by other arguments.
