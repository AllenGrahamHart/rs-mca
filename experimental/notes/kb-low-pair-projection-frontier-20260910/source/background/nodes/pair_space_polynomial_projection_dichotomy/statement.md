# A Pair Space Has A Short Normal Or A Generically Full Projection

Status: PROVED. Independent mathematical review remains due.

Let V be an s-dimensional vector space over ANY field F, and let
W subset V x V have dimension r and codimension c=2s-r. Let
pi_z(a,b)=a+z*b, with z an indeterminate. Exactly one alternative holds:

1. pi_z(W tensor F(z))=V tensor F(z). Then c<=s and all but at most
   c finite gamma in F satisfy pi_gamma(W)=V. For c=0 none fail.
2. The generic projection is proper. There is a nonzero polynomial
   normal ell(z)=ell0+z*ell1+...+z^d*ell_d in V*[z] satisfying

       ell(z)(a+z*b)=0 in F[z] for EVERY(a,b) in W.             (NORMAL)

   Choose the least possible degree d. Its d+1 coefficient functionals
   are linearly independent, and d+2<=c. If the SUM of W's component
   images is all V, then d>=1. Thus e=d+1 obeys2<=e<=c-1.

The degree of(NORMAL) as a parameter hypersurface in(z,y) is e=d+1.
For an affine pair family contained in(a0,b0)+W, this gives the ENTIRE
pair-line identity ell(z)(a-a0+z*(b-b0))=0, with the same offsets
for every pair. It is not merely a pointwise assertion at chosen labels.

This is finite-dimensional algebra, not a bound on source cardinality.
F(z) is used to test formal rank; no challenge field or denominator is
replaced. The regular alternative is not paid by this theorem.
