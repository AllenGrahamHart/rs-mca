# Audit

Minimality is over ALL nonzero polynomial normals, not merely a selected
ansatz. If their coefficient span has dimension t<=d, the restricted
t-column pencil gives a strictly shorter cofactor normal, a contradiction.
The annihilator vectors then give c>=d+2 with no characteristic assumption.

For the regular case, the map A is injective on ker(B-gamma*A), not
necessarily on all of T. This is sufficient for the exact dual dimension
identity. One nonzero symbolic minor controls all exceptional labels;
sampling finite field values would be insufficient and is not used.

The bounded checker tests polynomial minors, exact kernel equations,
sharp chain examples and a diagonal example with c exceptional slopes.
These are algebra controls, not realized unsafe MCA sources or a proof
by enumeration. Independent mathematical review remains due.
