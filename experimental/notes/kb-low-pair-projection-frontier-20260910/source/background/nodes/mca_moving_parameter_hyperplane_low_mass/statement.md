1<=T<=d,m=K+d<=n, a degree-<K polynomial space V of dimension s,
and fixed degree-<K offsets a0,b0. Choose independent coefficient
functionals ell0,...,ell_(e-1) on V. Put

    L_z(y)=sum_(i=0)^(e-1) z^i*ell_i(y), Q(z,y)=L_z(y).

For DISTINCT finite labels gamma choose pairs(a_gamma,b_gamma) in

defect counts1<=tau_gamma<=T. Evaluation on V must be nonzero on each

    L_z((a_gamma-a0)+z*(b_gamma-b0))=0 in F[z].      (LINE)

Pointwise vanishing on F, or vanishing at just the selected gamma, is
not substituted for this identity. Minimization is not required here.

There are at most K-s+e exceptional original labels. The others satisfy

    sum tau_gamma <= floor(e*(n)_falling_s /
        [s*(m-T)*product_(j=1)^(s-2)(d-T+j)]).       (DEGREE)

right-side floor plus T*(K-s+e). Every common ordered tuple has at
most e label owners, not one separately funded budget per moving carrier.
