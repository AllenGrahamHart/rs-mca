# Audit and provenance

This generalizes the fixed-divisor method of the already proved
homogeneous-level supplier: two monic integral equations replace its
product identity, and a finite-fiber argument replaces two linear
coordinates. Upper AND lower valuation bounds are essential.

Standard inputs, read directly on 2026-09-07:

- [Stacks, Lemma 29.55.11, tag 035S](https://stacks.math.columbia.edu/tag/035S):
  normalization is finite for a finite-type curve over a field.
- [Stacks, Proposition 53.10.4, tag 0C6U](https://stacks.math.columbia.edu/tag/0C6U):
  the smooth geometric genus-zero completion is P^1 after adjoining a
  rational point. All chosen coordinates and boundary points descend
  to one finite extension.
- The function-field/projective-curve and constant-function facts are
  already sourced in the required supplier, Stacks tags 0BY1 and 0366.

The unit need not be a coordinate or an unnormalized-ring unit. Its
fibers on a curve are finite, sufficient for the dimension argument even
in inseparable cases. Auxiliary evaluations construct a rational map on
the coefficient component; they are not code-domain genericity premises.
All exceptional pairs and divisor patterns remain in the degree cover.

The explicit three-boundary cubic is singular and outside the earlier
graph/affine-product classes. Both cuspidal and nodal one-boundary
examples reject expanding the theorem to every singular cubic.

No S-unit theorem, numerical height/rank count, formal certification or
upstream acceptance is claimed. The 500-term arithmetic does not prove
the geometry. Independent external hand review remains due.
