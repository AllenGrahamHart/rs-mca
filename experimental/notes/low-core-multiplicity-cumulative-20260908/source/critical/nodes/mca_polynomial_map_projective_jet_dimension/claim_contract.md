identity over F(X), with primitive A,B and maximum degree e>=2.
The same kernel dimension d controls input dimension 2s-d and family
dimension ceil(d/e). The input degree grows to K+h and the LIST
agreement must be at least K+h. Height bounds on primitive kernel
equations use their highest binary part c*(A Y+B Z)^e.

must not be substituted into this theorem. The finite-singularity
nodal/cuspidal class remains separate.

or p>=K. Auxiliary points are chosen in the algebraic closure, not assumed
generic on the code domain. Rational coefficient zeros/poles are fixed
exclusions for that dimension argument only.

not assumed linear or conical. Its projective closure is contained in the
homogenized equations; equality of their loci is neither used nor asserted.

original degree-<K first component and retains degree e^(s-ceil(s/e)).

the moving-projection extension instead retains degree e^(2s-d-r)
and applies LIST at the larger input degree K+h.

output carrier and the SAME map degree. It is not applied to unrelated
carriers or unequal-degree leading terms.
