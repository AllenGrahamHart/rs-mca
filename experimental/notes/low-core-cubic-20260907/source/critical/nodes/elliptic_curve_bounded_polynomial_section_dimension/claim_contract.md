# Dimension claim contract

- Object: reduced bounded polynomial-pair coefficient locus over an
  algebraically closed constant field, not a finite list or slope count.
- Curve: fixed total-degree-three equation over k(X), with smooth
  geometric projective completion; k has characteristic zero or >3.
- Conclusion: every component has dimension <=1; dimension zero if
  the associated elliptic surface has a singular fiber. Nonconstant j
  is a sufficient, not necessary, test for the latter condition.
- The surface is relatively minimal. Discriminant zeros in an
  arbitrary nonminimal equation are not a rigidity certificate.
- Polynomial bounds are arbitrary but fixed and finite. Restricting
  to affine coefficient subspaces preserves the dimension bound.
- X-specializations may be singular. No RS-domain assumption occurs.
- Classical surface and finite-presentation inputs are explicitly
  attributed in `audit.md`; there are no unresolved required premises.
- No transformed-coordinate height, finite pair cap, source-coverage
  assertion, original endpoint or prize conclusion follows alone.
