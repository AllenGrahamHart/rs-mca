# Contract

Input: an original weighted MCA source, primitive shared s>=4 carrier,
full-generic pair rank2s-3, actual normalization nu<=H, and characteristic
zero or p>D. Parameters R>=d>t and v>=0 give the original positive N,A corridor.

Output: an exact weighted paid-exit inequality using one generic-exception
budget and the branch-weighted normalization-crossing budget. Intersections
are allowed. Degree-band and quotient-endpoint envelopes are complete
upper bounds, not assertions that every enclosed degree pair is attainable.

C,F,T must already bound their exact hereditary original-owner classes.
No geometric free-selection denominator, terminal affordability, full source
budget or extension-field reinterpretation is assumed.
Falsifier: a missing actual crossing, an incorrect branch-to-coordinate
conversion, or a failure of the positive-increment incidence bound.
