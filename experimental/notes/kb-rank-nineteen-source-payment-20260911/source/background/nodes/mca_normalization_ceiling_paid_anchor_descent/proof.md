# Keep The Ceiling On Ordinary Children And Pay Its Failures

The required degree ledger gives D=nu*eta, eta>=s-1 and, at an actual
inner anchor, nu_child=nu*mu. The high-normalization event is exactly
mu>=floor(H/nu)+1. For s>=4 the image lies in ambient dimension at least3,
so the low-degree branch-budget theorem applies. Its coordinate count
includes nu times all geometric BRANCHES, not nu times the centre count.
Characteristic p>D implies p>eta, including every descendant.

The full-generic anchor supplier bounds its bad set E by kappa+3.
Outside E every joint evaluation is rank two and remains full generic.
Inside E there can be a generic rank-one child or an unrestricted
rank-two nonregular child. No generic rank-zero coordinate exists.

Let Z be the high-normalization set, with |Z|<=B_geo.
At x outside E union Z, the original child weight is at most C.
At x in E it is at most F; at x outside E but in Z it is at most T.
Thus each coordinate is paid by

    C + 1_E(x)*max(F-C,0) + 1_Z(x)*max(T-C,0).

This remains an upper bound at E intersect Z. Sum the SAME original
nonnegative owner weight at every remaining joint agreement. Each pair
has at least A such agreements, proving (PAID).
There is no (A-|E union Z|) denominator, no original weight reset, and no
requirement that auxiliary geometric centres be legal source anchors.

## Uniform Degree Envelope

Fix nu and h. As eta increases, the genus binomial is nondecreasing.
When centres are possible, b0 increases only up to max(1,h-2).
For h=2 that value is1. For h>=3 the integer ratio
b/binom(b+h-1,2) is nondecreasing for1<=b<=h-2:
the successive difference has the sign of
(h-1)*(h-2)-b*(b+1), which is positive for b<h-2.
Thus beta is nondecreasing in eta, including the initial zero region.
For actual D<=D_hi, replace eta=D/nu by floor(D_hi/nu).
The possible nu range follows from nu<=H and eta>=s-1.
No divisibility assumption is needed for this over-enclosure.

For exact finite compilation, partition nu by BOTH constant quotients
floor(H/nu) and floor(D_hi/nu). Within each integer interval,
h,eta,b0 and floor(genus*b0/charge) are constant; beta is nu times
a fixed nonnegative integer. The upper nu endpoint pays the whole interval.
An independent full integer-nu scan can certify this quotient compression.

The endpoint envelope in kappa,v follows from ratios of affine functions
with positive denominator. B_geo is constant over each degree band;
the kappa+3 generic-exception term is affine. At fixed kappa the limit
as v grows is C. At v=0 the ratio is monotone or constant in kappa.
Full shared gcds lower kappa and preserve nonnegative unused slack,
so prefix maxima over all preceding bands are required.

## History

The low-ceiling path uses only rank-two generic children until its terminal.
Taking an F or T price is a paid exit to an already proved hereditary class;
it is not followed by an unsupported re-entry under the ceiling.
Consequently a low terminal at shared dimension3 still has all eight
actual rank-two regular ancestors when starting from dimension11.
The old small-normalization terminal theorem can be used at that exact
history. This is not a uniform terminal assertion without its source gates.
