## Original Line Owners

The scalar parameterization of a primitive pair line is injective on its
original complete-core union C. The proved dimension-one affine bound
therefore gives the integer pair cap
floor((S+h-e)/(d+1-t+h)). Good anchors do not change that ratio.

For each represented pair, original selected defects outside C are
disjoint across its labels and number at most e. Inside C, compatibility
with the primitive row makes any defect's original finite label a function
of the coordinate ALONE. Because the original assignment gives a label
to just one pair, each inside coordinate is charged at most once across
the whole line, regardless of t. This is the same global inside-charge
argument as the rational-compression supplier, applied to one pencil.
For constant direction there is instead at most one preferred finite
label, whose total raw charge is at most t. If the preferred direction
is infinite, there is no such finite charge.

Thus the bounds are t+m*e and |C|+m*e=R+J+(m-1)*e respectively.
Same-pair ownership also gives m*c_t. This proves (LINE).
It sharpens the older safe but looser t*|C| charge; that older bound
is not refuted.

For e<=c_t, the ratio is
1+(c_t-e)/(d+1-t+h), nonincreasing in h. On[l,u] use l and any source
gate valid throughout that interval. The count m satisfies
1<=m<=floor((S+l-g-1)/(d+1-t+l)), and then e<=e_max.
Each weight formula is nondecreasing in e for m>=1, so e_max gives
a rigorous upper bound. No actual line population is invented.

with dim V=c, full-carrier rigidity forces constant direction. Its
complete-core union scalar LIST and one preferred-slope charge give the
whole-constant price. This does not require actual filling of V.

Otherwise c>=2 and some two direction pairs have nonzero determinant
of degree at most2D. All rank-two good sections have pair dimension c-2.

(N-2D)/(A-2D). Its maximum over v>=0 is attained at v0, giving
(R-D+1)/(d-D+1-t). This increases with D in the printed positive corridor.
For c2 the child has one possible pair. For c3 it is an affine pair line;
its direction vanishes at the anchor, so its primitive height is at most
D-1. The line price is uniform over all actual such children.
