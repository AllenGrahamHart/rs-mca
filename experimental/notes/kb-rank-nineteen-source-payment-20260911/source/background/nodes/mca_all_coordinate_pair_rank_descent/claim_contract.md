# Contract

Object: original finite-label MCA pair families with fixed nonnegative raw
owner weights. Input: primitive shared carrier V of dimension s,degree D,
W subset V squared of rank2s-c with0<=c<s, and the printed N,A,v corridor.
No actual filling of W is assumed. Generic projection is a separate guard.

Output: all-coordinate U and G weighted inequalities, exact child dimensions,
nested exceptional-coordinate budgets and nonincreasing primitive degree excess.
The transcendental z is a proof device, not a slope or coefficient-field change.
A rank-two child is not assumed generically full.
Terminal prices and any official source budget are outside this generic claim.

Falsifier: a declared space whose child ranks or exceptional sets violate
the inequalities, or a negative-slack normalization of an actual source.
The proof is uniform; small controls only check implementation cases.
