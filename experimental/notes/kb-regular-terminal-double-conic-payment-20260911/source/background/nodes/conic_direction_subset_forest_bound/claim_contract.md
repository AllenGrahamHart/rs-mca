# Scope Contract

- Odd characteristic and a NONDEGENERATE ternary quadratic form.
- M>=2 distinct ACTUAL points and groups on pairwise distinct null lines.
- Each group contributes a TREE, not all its clique edges.
- Subsets reconnect the selected points with a new tree before averaging.
- K_s is a proved sufficient upper bound, not an exact extremal graph number.
- The incidence dual is checked for every integer occupancy0..q and uses
  nonnegative fibre sizes, l_i<=nu, a>=1 and b>=0.
- There is no random survival hypothesis, source-line cover or MCA payment.

Falsifier: a qualifying line-tree graph with a triangle or more than two
common neighbours of a triple; an exact subset average violating its K_s;
or an admissible dual violating the stated fibre-incidence bound.
