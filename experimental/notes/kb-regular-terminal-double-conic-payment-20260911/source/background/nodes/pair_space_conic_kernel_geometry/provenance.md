# Provenance

Developed locally after the cf6cd480 branch/genus publication. The earlier
double-conic pilot was not included in that publication. This node now
records its geometric claims and the missing original-field justifications.

The [Columbia conic notes](https://www.math.columbia.edu/~dejong/reu/doku.php%3Fid=conics.html)
were consulted for standard conic background, not used as a proof of the
new kernel or source statements. The relevant elementary arguments are
written here. The normalized degree calculation uses the local required
supplier, whose standard references include Stacks0C1R/0BXX/0BYA.
