# Scope Contract

- Original field F has odd characteristic; no source-field extension.
- U has dimension3, full polynomial gcd1 and actual degree D.
- The evaluation image is a geometric conic with an F-point.
- The operator pair space has function-field rank2 and is pencil-free over F.
- Kernel-image degree chi is in2..4; only chi2 gives an isomorphism and
  the two-base-point bound. Generic conic evaluation is not enough.
- The exact collision height nu is claimed only for a non-eigen direction
  with an ACTUAL joint zero at an F-coordinate.
- Geometric base points are counted with multiplicity before restriction
  to actual original-field coordinates. No generic-fibre substitution.
- No pair census, original weight, source payment or Prize result is asserted.

Falsifier: a qualifying operator with kernel degree outside2..4, failure
of the chi2 kernel isomorphism, or an actual non-eigen collision of different
primitive height. The chi3/4 fixtures guard against the stronger false scope.
