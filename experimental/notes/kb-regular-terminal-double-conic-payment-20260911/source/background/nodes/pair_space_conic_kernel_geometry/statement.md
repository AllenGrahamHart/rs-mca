# Evaluation Conics And Their Kernel-Direction Curves

Let F have odd characteristic. Let U subset F[X] have dimension3, gcd1,
and actual maximum degree D. Let T be an F-endomorphism and assume the
pair space {(y,Ty):y in U} has function-field rank2 and contains NO
F-two-plane of function-field rank1 (pencil-free). Suppose its projective
evaluation image C is a geometrically integral conic with an F-point.
C is the algebraic image of P1, not just the finite attained point set.

Then U=span_F(P^2,PQ,Q^2) for coprime P,Q in F[X], with
nu=max(deg P,deg Q)>=1 and D=2nu. No extension of the original field is
needed. The evaluation map P1->C has finite fibre size at most nu.

At rank-two p=[ell] in C, define d(p)=ker(ell,ell*T) in P(U).
Its image K is geometrically integral, nondegenerate and birational to C.
Its equation degree chi belongs to {2,3,4}. It is NOT always a conic.

If chi=2, d extends to an isomorphism C->K. Its cross-product expression
has base divisor of degree2, so at most two geometric rank-one evaluation
points exist. All nonbase points have DISTINCT null directions on a
nonsingular ternary quadratic form over F.

For an F-rational rank-one point p0, H0=ker ell0 is T-invariant, and the
extended d(p0) lies in P(H0). Hence joint evaluation restricted to H0 is
noninjective at at most ONE other nonbase point of C. At a second rational
rank-one point p1, H0 intersection ker ell1 is an F-eigenvector line.

For ANY eta2 kernel curve, if a non-eigen direction y causes an actual
joint collision at an F-point, the primitive rational pair (y,Ty) has
height exactly nu. This claim is not made for noncolliding directions.
Multiplication by a scalar polynomial and a fixed invertible F pair change
preserve this primitive height.
