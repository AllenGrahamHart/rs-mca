## Interpretation

The h=8 row is the first checked minimal-signature size where raw injectivity
fails after the h=5, h=6, and h=7 empty sweeps.  The failure is not a primitive
obstruction: it is exactly the cyclic coset partition and is already paid by
the existing pullback dictionary.

This sharpens the terminal proof target.  A plausible theorem should not be
phrased as global top-`h-1` signature injectivity.  It should be phrased as
one of:

```text
1. every top-(h-1) signature collision is paid by the full strip; or
2. after the full strip, the primitive row-wise split-pair residue is <= n^3;
   in the high-q form, it may vanish entirely.
```

X19 supports the second formulation and gives a concrete paid counterexample
to the first, overly strong raw-injectivity formulation.
