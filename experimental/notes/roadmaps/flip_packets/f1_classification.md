## Referee Argument

The children price the three branches after a slope has already been assigned
to one of them. They do not prove that these branches are exhaustive. The cited
S6 note explicitly labels this part as a classification conjecture and gives
the route:

```text
the classification is "which K the pair's pencil data generates" --
a Galois-descent stratification of pairs
```

That Galois-descent stratification is own mathematical content. A fourth class
of genuinely `F`-valued bad slopes, not pole-type and not subfield-confined,
would require a new ledger column. Conversely, proving that no fourth class
exists is exactly the missing theorem.

So this packet certifies TRUE RED. The node is not merely missing a manifest
edge: its core trichotomy remains open.

## Remaining Proof

Prove a Galois-descent classification for the alignment pencil:

1. attach to each bad F-valued slope the field generated by the pair's pencil
   data;
2. show B-generated data gives B-rational slopes;
3. show non-B-generated bad slopes above reserve either arise from the
   extension-pole/list import or are confined to an intermediate field;
4. rule out a genuinely F-valued, non-pole, non-confined residual class.

The tower subcase may become moot if the official admissible family excludes
non-generating rows, but that would only delete case (iii); it would not prove
the trichotomy in the live statement.
