## Arithmetic

The serial replay runs eight new checks and six inherited resource/pencil
checks. Primary and separately implemented audits are both included.

- Coupled frontier:108 height bands,252 compression boxes,58 original
  source boxes,2448 chosen LIST transitions,22 rejected corruptions.
- Integer compression:172 boxes in four degree/cutoff cases,860 chosen
  LIST transitions,23 rejected scope/degree/cardinality/coverage/price
  corruptions. The independent auditor imports neither primary code nor
  the LIST compiler and reconstructs the final rational floor separately.
- Inherited minimum envelopes:765 whole-interval branch gates,512
  coefficient transfers,24 wrong endpoint floors rejected.
- Inherited HIGH44 pencil gate:582 additional LIST transitions and10
  rejected corruptions. Original raw-resource checks are also replayed.

All rational comparisons retain exact numerators and denominators.
Terminal floors printed in the notes are display-only; they are not
substituted into the source payment. No floating-point feasibility
calculation, sample of field elements, or large source enumeration is
used to establish the bounds.

## Limits

The three small algebraic controls and52 small integer-mass multisets
are fixtures, not full-code-bad MCA realizations. The universal geometric
and original-ownership assertions require the hand proofs. Checksums,
status badges, mutation tests and finite arithmetic do not replace
independent mathematical review. Other inherited arithmetic, global
software, the full critical DAG and any cross-repository owner compiler
are not revalidated by this standalone replay.

Both normal and optimized (`-O`) replays passed at publication. Measured
full-run elapsed times were4.09/5.16seconds and maximum RSS25356/25872KiB,
under RAMguard tiny. These timings are observations, not runtime guarantees.

Normal and optimized (`-O`) replay commands are in the README. Run them
serially with a256MiB/60second guard on constrained machines. No external
packages, Modal account, network, paid computation or compute request
are required. The runtime uses temporary copies and writes no source file.
