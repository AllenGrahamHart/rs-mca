# Integer Compression Masses Admit A Two-Level Affine Bound

Status: PROVED; independent external mathematical review remains due.

In the source-bound compression setup of mca_compression_fibre_raw_mass,
let actual occupied fibre masses x_c be integers in[L,Z], with L>=1,
Z<=S=n-K+1 and sum x_c<=S. Let w(x)=(S-x)*L_list(x) bound each
fibre's nonpreferred original raw weight.

For integers T>=L,H>=0 with (H+1)*(T+1)>S, choose alpha,beta>=0
such that w(x)<=alpha*x on the small-mass interval x<=T and
w(x)<=alpha*x+beta on the large-mass interval x>=T+1. Then

    total original raw<=t weight <=t+alpha*S+H*beta.

There is at most one common preferred finite slope, charged by t ONCE.

3*349526=1048578>S. The real mass average is not substituted for an
