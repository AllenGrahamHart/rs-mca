# Compression Fibres Share One Shifted Complete-Core Budget

Status: PROVED. Independent external mathematical review remains due.

Fix a subfamily of one original weighted MCA source as defined by
mca_weighted_pencil_raw_mass. Original degrees are<K and each complete
joint core has size>=A. Suppose 0<=a<K distinct good anchors are shared by
all its cores and its pairs lie in

    f_*+H_a*((delta tensor U) direct_sum F z),
    U subset F[X]_{<K-a}, delta in F^2 nonzero, z in U x U,
    z not in delta tensor U.

Quotienting by delta tensor U partitions ACTUAL pairs into fibres c.
Let C_c be each fibre's complete-core union and x_c=|C_c|-K+1. If
A>K-1, then every occupied fibre has x_c>=A-K+1>0 and

    sum_c x_c<=n-K+1.                                  (PACK)

If all x_c<=Z and scalar LIST caps L(x) apply on
(length,degree,agreement)=(x+K-1-a,K-a,A-a), on the fibre's ACTUAL
affine scalar span or a proved enclosing scalar space, then its original raw weight
is at most

    t+sum_c (n-K+1-x_c)*L(x_c)
      <=t+(n-K+1)*sup_legal_x ((n-K+1-x)*L(x)/x).        (OWNER)

There is at most ONE preferred finite slope for the entire terminal.
Here legal x lies in[A-K+1,min(Z,n-K+1)], so the weighted ratio is
nonnegative and the positive shifted masses may be bounded by(PACK).
Neither the tuple resource nor this common preferred charge is spent
once per fibre. The bound also holds for an empty terminal. No scalar
LIST, fibre count or anchor sequence is itself an original slope count.
