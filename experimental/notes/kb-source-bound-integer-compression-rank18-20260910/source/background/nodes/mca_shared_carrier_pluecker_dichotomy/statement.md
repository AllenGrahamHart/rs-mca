# Sparse Rank-One Children Or One-Sided Compression

Status: PROVED. Independent external mathematical review remains due.

Let U be a polynomial space of dimension s>=3 and degree<K on N distinct
field points. Let W subset U x U have dimension s+1 and suppose
N-K+1>2(K-1). For a good coordinate x, joint evaluation on W has rank2;
its kernel K_x has dimension s-1. Dividing X-x embeds that kernel into
the square of the scalar carrier U_x of dimension s-1.

Exactly the following exhaustive alternative applies:

1. Some coefficient of the bivariate exterior product p_y wedge p_x
   is nonzero, where p_x=ev_0,x wedge ev_1,x in Lambda^2 W*. At most
   2K-2 good coordinates have function-field-rank-one children.
2. The exterior product vanishes identically and
   W=(delta tensor U) direct_sum F z for a constant nonzero delta in F^2.
   Every good child has constant function-field direction delta.

In the first case every rank-one child is constant-direction by equality
rigidity. The bound counts GOOD coordinates only. Bad evaluations and
earlier anchors are not silently assigned to this exception set.

This is an algebraic classification of an actual enclosing direction
space, not a claim that all its points are represented explanations.
It counts no original labels and supplies no Prize payment by itself.
