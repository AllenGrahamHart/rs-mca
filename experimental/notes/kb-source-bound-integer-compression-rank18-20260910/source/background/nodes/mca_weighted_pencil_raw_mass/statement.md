# Original Raw Weights Through Anchors And Pencil Sections

Status: PROVED. Independent external mathematical review remains due.

Fix integers K>=1,1<=A<=n,t>=1, one received pair(u,v) on n distinct
field points, and an original finite
label assignment gamma -> (a_gamma,b_gamma), and selected scalar agreement
supports for a_gamma+gamma*b_gamma. For t>=1 retain labels with positive
original raw<=t. Give each represented pair f the weight omega_t(f), the
SUM of those original raw values assigned to it. Suppose its complete
joint core H_f has at least A points. Weights never change in a section.

For any subfamily in a dimension-v affine polynomial pencil with primitive
height h and union C of its COMPLETE joint cores, set e=n-|C|. If the
original degree is<K and A>K-h-1, its weight sum is at most

    t+e*((n-e-K+1)/(A-K+1))^v                   if h=0,
    t*(n-e)+e*((n-e-K+h+1)/(A-K+h+1))^v         if h>=1.

Empty families have weight zero. Common good anchors may be divided from
pair differences and deleted from this auxiliary scalar count; the ratios
above stay unchanged. Neither receiver nor original source is redefined.

The shared-carrier anchor bound extends to these nonnegative weights:
at s<r<=2s and A>K+s-r, a uniform child weight cap L gives total at most
(n-K-s+r)/(A-K-s+r)*L. With 0<=a<K distinct common good anchors already
present, a function-field-rank-two r-space additionally has weighted
factor (n-2K+a+2)/(A-2K+a+2), provided the denominator is positive,
and children of affine dimension r-2. A zero-dimensional pair has weight
at most n-A. Prior anchors are included in all bad-coordinate counts.

These bounds concern original raw mass, not numbers of arbitrary affine
hull points, histories or slopes. A consumer must restore its exact
original truncated-raw identity and add near only once.
