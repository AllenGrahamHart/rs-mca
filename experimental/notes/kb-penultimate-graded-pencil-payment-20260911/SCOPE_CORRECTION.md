# Explicit Residual-Source Antecedent

The preceding normalization manifest at commitbf84251a contains

```text
terminal_bound_scope:
Pencil-free regular3 terminals after proved original large-pencil source alternatives are removed
```

Its README, upstream handoff and standalone statement did not repeat that
qualification clearly enough. A local L_t claim must not silently apply
to a source already covered by a whole-source pencil alternative.

The corrected rule is:

1. If an already-paid whole-source pencil alternative occurs, apply its
   whole-source cap.
2. Otherwise its complement gates give hereditary line/plane capacities,
   so the local terminal and prefix L_t prices apply.
3. Combine whole-source alternatives by maximum, not addition.

The new original maximal-pencil source theorem includes both branches.
It therefore has no unproved avoidance assumption.

The inherited supplier's statement.md, claim_contract.md and node.json
are amended in this packet. Their old/new hashes are recorded in
SOURCE_MANIFEST.scope_amendments. All its other files, including numerical
certificates, proof and verifiers, are unchanged. Older published packets
remain immutable. The two new consumers print the antecedent explicitly.

This is a correction to standalone scope presentation, not an exhibited
official counterexample or an unrestricted rank19 proof. The old public
claim should be read with this qualification.
