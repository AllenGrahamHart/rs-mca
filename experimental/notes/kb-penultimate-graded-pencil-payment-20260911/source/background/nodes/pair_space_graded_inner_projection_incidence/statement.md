# Price Actual Regular Anchors By Their Child Image Degrees

Let V be a full-gcd1 four-dimensional polynomial carrier of actual degree
D=nu*d, with nondegenerate rational image in P3. Let W subset V x V have
dimension5 and full generic projection. Assume W is PENCIL-FREE: no
F-plane of pair directions has function-field rank one. Characteristic0
or p>d is required.

Actual pairs in an affine translate of W have fixed nonnegative owner
weights at most c each and complete joint cores of size at least A on
an N-point domain. Assume N>=A>D. For every retained regular anchor, suppose
the ACTUAL child count has certified bounds b0 for a birational inner
centre and C_eta for an inner-image degree eta>=2. Put

    T=max_(eta>=2) (eta-1)*max(C_eta-b0,0).

If b0 and the C_eta are finite and nonnegative, the total ORIGINAL weight Omega satisfies

    Omega <= c*((N-D)*b0+D*T)/(A-D).               (GRADED ANCHOR)

The child bounds concern actual subsets, not occupancies of their formal
enclosures. No coordinate or owner is reassigned by a geometric projection.
The finite consumer supplies all child bounds; they are not unproved leaves.
