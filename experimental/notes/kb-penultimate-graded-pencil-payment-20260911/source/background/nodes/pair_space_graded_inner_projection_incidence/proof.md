# Sum One Shared Multiplicity Budget Instead Of Deleting Every Bad Centre

The regular-anchor supplier applied to degree bound D+1, shared dimension4
and pair dimension5 gives an excluded set of size b<=D. Outside it,
the actual affine child has a regular pair/shared enclosure3/3. Every
F-plane of child directions lies in W, so pencil-freeness is hereditary.
The enclosure is therefore neither whole constant nor a rank-two space
with a rank-one plane. It admits the original-field operator chart.

Count good joint agreements with the ORIGINAL weights:

    (A-b)*Omega <= sum_(good x) Omega_x.

For a birational inner centre, Omega_x<=c*b0. At a nonbirational centre
P of inner degree mu and image degree eta, Omega_x<=c*C_eta. Its actual
coordinate fibre has size at most nu*b_P<=nu*m_P. Thus

    sum_(good x) Omega_x
      <=c*(N-b)*b0+c*sum_P nu*m_P*max(C_eta-b0,0).

Here d=m_P+mu*eta. The required multiplicity theorem and exact ratio give

    nu*m_P*max(C_eta-b0,0)
      <= (D/G)*T*binom(m_P+mu-1,2),
    G=binom(d-2,2).

Summing the ONE shared genus budget bounds the last sum by D*T.
If G=0 there are no nonbirational centres and the same nonnegative bound
holds without division. Singular birational centres are covered by b0;
they are not silently treated as smooth.

Finally ((N-b)*b0+D*T)/(A-b) increases with b for b<A, because N>=A
and all costs are nonnegative. Replacing b by D proves GRADED ANCHOR.
Unlike the coarse exclusion recipe, this retains every regular coordinate
and only charges its positive excess over the birational-child price.
