# A Maximal Nonconstant Pencil Survives Every Regular Anchor

Start with a regular rank19 prefix whose shared and pair dimensions
are s and2s-3, s>=4, and whose pair enclosure contains a nonconstant
pencil of dimension s-1. Product growth makes this dimension maximal.

At every retained regular anchor its pencil section has dimension s-2,
while the shared and pair dimensions become s-1 and2(s-1)-3. The
same hypothesis is therefore preserved, including after division by
the actual anchor locator and full gcd. This is a statement about
every regular child, not the existence of a favorable generic path.

After s-3 anchors the enclosure has dimensions3/3 and contains a
nonconstant pencil plane. A whole function-field-rank-one3/3 enclosure
would have nonconstant rational direction, contradicting product growth:
three scalar dimensions would need at least four shared dimensions.
The terminal consequently has rank two and lies in the existing
rational-plane terminal class.

Original affine subfamilies need not span or populate their enclosures.
The finite consumer applies the already proved original weighted-anchor
factors to these actual subfamilies. No normalization cap is required
for this maximal-pencil descent.
