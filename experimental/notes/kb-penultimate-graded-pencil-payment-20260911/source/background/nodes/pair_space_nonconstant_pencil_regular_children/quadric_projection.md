# The Only Possible Inner Degree Jump Is At A Cone Vertex

In the5/4 situation take a basis u,v of T and put U=P*T+Q*T.
Product growth gives dim U=3 or4.

If dim U=4, the four products Pu,Pv,Qu,Qv form a basis of V.
The projective carrier image C lies on the smooth split quadric

    X0*X3-X1*X2=0.

This remains true after removing a full gcd and homogenizing to the
actual carrier degree. Nondegeneracy of C follows from independence
of the carrier basis, over F and after extension of constants.

If dim U=3, the intersection P*T intersect Q*T is one-dimensional.
Choose nonzero a,b in T with Q*a=P*b. Coprimality gives a=P*g,b=Q*g.
Thus T=g*span(P,Q), and U=g*span(P^2,PQ,Q^2). Adding any fourth
basis element of V places C on the quadric cone

    X0*X2-X1^2=0,

whose unique geometric vertex is p_*=[0:0:0:1]. This is valid also
in characteristic2: the conic base is smooth and the only singular
point of the cone is the displayed vertex.

## Projection At A Smooth Quadric Point Is Birational On C

Let p be a smooth point of either quadric, lying on C. Write the quadric
form as q and its polar form as B. On a line through p, with parameter
vector v modulo p,

    q(p+t*v)=t*B(p,v)+t^2*q(v).

Away from the tangent plane B(p,v)=0 and the usual affine-chart
denominators, the second intersection point is given rationally by
t=-B(p,v)/q(v). Thus projection from p has a rational inverse on the
quadric's open set off its tangent plane. This argument works in every
characteristic and does not infer separability from a point count.

The nondegenerate curve C is not contained in that tangent plane, so
restriction gives a rational inverse on a dense open subset of C.
It is therefore birational on C even if C itself is singular at p.
Equivalently a general secant through p is not a ruling line.

## What Happens At The Cone Vertex

At p_*, all functionals on U vanish. If an ACTUAL regular coordinate x
maps there, the two components of every pair in S vanish at x, so
S subset W_x. The preceding proof shows that this3/3 child has
function-field rank two. Thus it contains a nonconstant pencil plane,
regardless of the inner projection's degree.

The original degree-ledger theorem gives nu_child=nu_parent*mu.
Every nonvertex inner anchor has mu1. Only the cone vertex can increase
normalization degree, and its child is precisely in the rational-plane
terminal class. No exceptional coordinate or original owner is deleted.
