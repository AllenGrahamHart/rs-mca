# Pay The Last Anchor Uniformly Before Returning To The Original Source

After seven actual anchors, remove the full polynomial gcd of the shared
carrier. If its primitive degree is D=nu*d_image and z distinct remaining
domain roots were deleted, the unused-degree quantity

    v=J-8-D-z

is nonnegative. The parent has N=R+D+1+v and complete core at least
A=d0+D+1-t+v. The original per-pair raw weight is c_t=R-d0+t.
This is the same owner bound as before; no shortened raw value is introduced.

The assumed pencil-free parent makes every regular child pencil-free.
The child-count proofs in child_caps.md give a uniform birational cap b0
and image-degree caps C_eta. They retain actual subsets and varying
receivers. Define T=max_(eta>=2)(eta-1)*(C_eta-b0)_+.
All eta>=b0 have C_eta<=b0, so this is a finite maximum.

The graded-anchor theorem gives

    weight<=c_t*((R+1+v)*b0+D*T)/(d0+1-t+v).

The ratio decreases with v>=0 and increases with D. Insert D<=K=J1-8
and v=0. All26 profile/cutoff certificates prove

    c_t*((R+1)*b0+K*T)<(R+1)*L_t.

Division by d0+1-t proves the original penultimate allowance. This
covers EVERY degree and normalization parameter satisfying the printed
scope, not just values tested by the earlier endpoint probes.

The full fixed divisor at a child can remove more roots than the chosen
anchor. If its degree is nu*m and z_child actual distinct roots are
deleted, the new slack is v+nu*m-z_child>=0. Thus every child used above
has exactly the inherited full-gcd/slack contract; repeated and nondomain
roots have not been lost.

If every populated seven-anchor enclosure met this theorem's conditions,
the remaining seven original anchor factors and the original raw-resource
identity would pay the entire source. Excess therefore supplies a cutoff/
path with an unpaid penultimate enclosure. Either it contains a rank-one
F-plane of directions or its normalization exceeds H. In the latter case
the original dimension11 degree bound forces an ACTUAL degree jump among
the first seven anchors. The former is an enclosure property, not a claim
that its plane is populated by an actual pencil subfamily.

Neither this alternative nor the constant upper tail is asserted paid.

In the high-normalization alternative, the parent image degree is3..9:
nondegeneracy in P3 gives d_image>=3, while
10*(H+1)>J1-1>J1-8>=nu*d_image and nu>=H+1 give d_image<10.
At image degree3, every inner projection has mu1,m1 and child image2,
by3=m+mu*eta with m>=1,eta>=2. The high normalization there must
already have been created upstream; it cannot be charged as a new
nonbirational event at the last anchor.

## Why Pencil-Freeness Cannot Be Assumed One Step Earlier

At an earlier rank19 prefix with shared dimension s, the regular pair
enclosure has dimension2s-3. Every fixed scalar projection W->V has
kernel dimension at least s-3. For s>=5 this contains an F-plane whose
pair directions are constant and hence function-field rank one. Thus
the pencil-free parent condition is impossible at those earlier prefixes.
The present5/4 theorem cannot be iterated upstream by assuming the same
condition; a mixed-plane treatment or different source argument is needed.
