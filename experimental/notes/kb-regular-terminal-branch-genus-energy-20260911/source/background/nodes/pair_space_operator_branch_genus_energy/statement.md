# Actual Fibre Capacity And Plane Branches Bound Core Incidence

Status: PROVED locally; independent mathematical review remains due.

Let U be a three-dimensional polynomial space over a field F, with gcd1
and ACTUAL maximum degree D. Let T:U->U have at most one-dimensional
F-eigenspaces, and select exactly M distinct actual pairs f_*+(y,T(y)).
The fixed received pair and complete joint cores are those of the required
collision-interpolation theorem. Each core has size at least A on an
n-point domain. On the union E of F-eigenpolynomial roots, total actual
core incidence is at most B.

Let eta>=2 be the degree of the primitive equation of the projective plane
image of U. Set nu=D/eta and g=(eta-1)*(eta-2)/2. Then nu is a positive
INTEGER, the degree of the induced finite map to the image normalization.
For p on the geometric image let b_p be its number of geometric branches.

Outside E, let l_p count actual domain coordinates in the fibre p and r_p
be the largest agreeing group at one such coordinate. Then

    l_p<=nu*b_p,  sum_p binom(b_p,2)<=g,
    C=sum_p binom(r_p,2)<=binom(M,2)=P.

Consequently total remaining core incidence S satisfies

    S<=n/2+sqrt((n+2*nu*g)*(n/4+2*nu*C))
     <=n/2+sqrt((n+2*nu*g)*(n/4+2*nu*P)).          (BRANCH ENERGY)

In particular an M-subset is impossible if Z=2*(M*A-B)-n>0 and

    Z^2>(n+2*nu*g)*(n+8*nu*P).                   (STRICT PRICE)

The SAME actual pairs supply C. No possible-direction census or full-hull
occupation is substituted. Normalization degree alone is NOT a finite
fibre bound: the factor b_p is essential. Unibranch singularities and
ramification cost no additional distinct branches; inseparability is allowed.
This theorem changes no original owner weight and claims no MCA row closure.
