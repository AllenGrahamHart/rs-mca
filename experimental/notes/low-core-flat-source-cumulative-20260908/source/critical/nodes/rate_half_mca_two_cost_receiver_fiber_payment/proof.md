## 4. Finite Boxes And The Two-Cost Inequality

For each gate (J_min,L)=(21000,6000),(23000,8000), partition degrees
into consecutive width500 intervals J0..J1 ending at H. Partition

    B_L=min(beta_high(J0),
            max(beta0(J0),12*min(F(J0,a0,0),F(J0,a0,(1-kappa0)*a0)))),
    B_H=min(beta_high(J0),
            max(beta0(J0),12*min(F(J0,a0,kappa0*a0),F(J0,a0,kappa1*a0)))).
