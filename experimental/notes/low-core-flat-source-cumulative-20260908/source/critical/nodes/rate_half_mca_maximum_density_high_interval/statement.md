# Every normalized high-interval carrier is paid

Status: PROVED by the hand proof and exact finite arithmetic; external
independent review remains due.

Let |F|=2130706433^6, 65000<=J<=169999, and let D_0 be ANY set of
n=1048576+J distinct F-points. Let V have actual F-dimension eleven and
polynomial degree <J, h_* have degree <J, and (u,v) be any received pair.

    {x in D_0: V(x)=0, v(x)=0, u(x)=h_*(x)}=empty.

For each distinct finite gamma in Gamma, choose an explanation in h_*+V
and a size-m support, m=67472+J, on which u+gamma*v equals that
explanation. The support must be bad for (u,v) in the FULL degree-<J
polynomial pair code. No maximal-raw selection is required. Then

    |Gamma|+134944<=274929007493481160,
    floor(|F|/2^128)=274980728111395087,
    reserve>=51720617913927.                         (HIGH-INTERVAL)

There is NO fiber-size, flat-dimension, curve-cover, receiver-descent,
field-drop or numerical-flatness premise. A maximum-density proper flat
is selected from the actual carrier and ALL its possible dimensions are
accounted for in the proof.

This is a uniform normalized-source theorem, not an assertion that every
original received line has been transported to this source. To call the
left side an original-row count, establish exhaustion of post-near labels
and the original near allowance. Higher original ranks, lower normalized
J, ordinary LIST and the two full prize problems remain separate.
