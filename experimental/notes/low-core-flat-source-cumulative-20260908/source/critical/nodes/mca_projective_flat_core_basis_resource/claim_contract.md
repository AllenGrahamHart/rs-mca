# Exact Contract

- Object: distinct selected finite MCA slopes, in the unchanged field.
- Carrier: actual dimension s, fixed affine translate, degree <K.
- Supports: size m=K+d and bad in the full degree-<K pair code.
- Raw: minimum mismatch to b in the actual carrier on the chosen support;
  canonical maximum-raw selection is not required by this theorem.
- Structural premise: every j-dimensional subspace of V* contains
  nonzero evaluations at at most j*h original coordinates, 1<=j<s.
  Arc independence and fiber size <=h are sufficient, not necessary.
- Carrier zeros: the universally agreeing subset has size g and is
  SUBTRACTED in every core-basis factor. The global tuple budget retains z.
- Output: the exact (FLAT) inequality and the conditional-on-printed-
  parameters (MIN) corollary, both proved universally at those hypotheses.
- Resource ownership: within-record lower bounds combine by maximum;
  records share one disjoint global tuple budget.
- Nonclaims: no automatic arc property, small-fiber property, curve cover,
  original-source transport, original near, unrestricted endpoint or prize.

A falsifier is a selected source satisfying these hypotheses and exceeding
the printed tuple/count inequality. Distinct projective classes without
the higher-dimensional occupancy premise are NOT a falsifier; their omitted hypothesis is
tested explicitly in controls.md.
