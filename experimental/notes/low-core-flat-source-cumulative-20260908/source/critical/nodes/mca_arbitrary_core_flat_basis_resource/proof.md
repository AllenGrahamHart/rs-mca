# Proof Without Requiring The Chosen Flat To Maximize Density

The required density-aware completion proof already supplies the
interleaving construction, exact inside-extension identity and signed
elimination. Its maximum-flat premise is not imported: we reconstruct
the quotient and the bounds for the arbitrary complete H-flat here.

The evaluations span V* because M>K. The annihilator W of G has
dimension ell, and all its polynomials vanish on the b distinct points B.
Full-locator division is injective and gives degree <K-b. Outside B its
evaluations are nonzero: a zero there would place that H-evaluation in G,
contradicting completeness. The quotient therefore has rank ell and gap
M-K. A rank-i quotient flat lifts to a rank-(t+i) flat containing ALL B.
Its outside occupancy is at most u_(t+i)-b. Greedy extension gives Q_greedy.

Choose an ordered quotient basis and an ordered independent i-tuple T
inside B. Their span meets G exactly in span(T). If y_T of the b inside
points are outside that span, at least b-y_T inside coordinates remain
in every subsequent span. At a completion span of rank r<s, at most u_r
total H-coordinates are present, so there are at least M-u_r-y_T outside
choices. In reverse completion order these are d_v-y_T. They are positive:
d_v>=M-K+1+v>b. The binomial interleavings recover T and the first ell
outside positions, so no basis is counted twice. Different i are disjoint.

Exactly sum_T y_T=E_(i+1). Every inside rank-i span has at most c_i
points and at least i, giving the displayed bounds without dividing by
E_i. If E_i=0 the next count is also zero. Each R_k is positive decreasing
and convex on 0<=y<=b, so the supplier's tangent proof and telescoping
coefficients apply verbatim. Backward elimination uses the LOWER extension
ratio for a nonnegative tail and the UPPER ratio for a negative tail.
Smaller d_v, larger b endpoint and larger c_i give its BOX formula.
Only positive lower counts may be multiplied by a lower Q.

For the final density statement, a proper quotient i-flat contains at most
(t+i)h-b=i*h+(t*h-b)<=i*((t+1)h-b) coordinates. The root bound
K-b-ell+i divided by i is at most K-b-ell+1, since K-b>=ell.
The minimum of these two uniform density bounds is valid. The balanced
basis theorem can be used only AFTER checking its rank/length/density gate;
no unqualified product or unproved curvature is assumed.

This proof depends only on the geometry of H. G need not maximize density
on H or on the full evaluation domain. It changes no receiver, selected
explanation, minimizing pair, original slope or exceptional-label charge.
