# A Boundary Of Uniform Per-Record Core-Basis Payment

This is a limitation of using a universal per-record lower count with
the coarse global budget n_falling_12. It is NOT an unsafe MCA source,
an impossibility theorem for source-dependent incidence counting, or a
counterexample to either prize problem.

On a normalized KoalaBear row let n=1048576+J, m=67472+J and J>=11.
Choose a core H of size m-1 and A subset H of size a=J-10. Let P_A
be its locator and use the actual dimension-eleven degree-<J carrier

    V=span{1,P_A,X*P_A,...,X^9*P_A}.

The constant is independent of the ten locator multiples because a>0.
All carrier evaluations are nonzero. The points of A lie in one
projective evaluation fiber. An independent ordered eleven-tuple from H
can contain at most one A point. With b=|H minus A|=67481, therefore

    B_11(V,H)<=b_falling_11+11*a*b_falling_10
              =b_falling_10*(67471+11a).             (UPPER)

There is an actual full-code-bad raw-one record with this core. Choose
x_0 outside H; put (u,v)=(0,0) on H, (1,1) at x_0 and (0,1)
elsewhere. At gamma=-1 the zero explanation agrees on exactly
S=H union {x_0}. Neither received component on S can be a degree-<J
polynomial: it vanishes at at least J core points and is nonzero at x_0.
The minimum mismatch to V is exactly one, witnessed by the zero polynomial.
The universal carrier core is empty because 1 belongs to V.

Every independent twelve-tuple inside this selected support must contain
x_0; the others form a core basis. Thus its TOTAL independent incidence
tuple count equals 12*B_11(V,H), not just a count of one chosen subfamily.
