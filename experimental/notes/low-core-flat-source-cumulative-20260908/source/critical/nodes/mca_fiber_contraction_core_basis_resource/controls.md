# Controls

The primary checker uses fixed small actual polynomial carriers with
repeated projective fibers, computes the full locator quotient, and recounts
ordered bases before and after contraction. It checks actual rank, degree,
nonzero quotient evaluations and exact preservation of D.

The independent audit enumerates small integer partitions under the
proper-flat mass constraint. It checks the second moment and quadratic
endpoint inequalities, and reconstructs the second derivative from
polynomial multiplication rather than the displayed derivative formula.
These controls do not replace the universal proof or certify finite-field
realizability of arbitrary partitions used in the relaxation.
