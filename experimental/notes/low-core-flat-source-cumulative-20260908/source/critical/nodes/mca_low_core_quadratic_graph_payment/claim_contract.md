- Moving parabola: characteristic not two, common carrier s>=2,
  nonsingular projective conic over F(X), rank-one quadratic part,
  and NONCONSTANT quadratic direction in X. Its coefficient dimension
  is <=floor(s/2). Constant direction and unrelated carriers have
  explicit counterfamilies to that stronger dimension bound.
- Rational parameterization is an isomorphism of bounded coefficient
  loci after clearing fixed denominators. It supplies dimension only.
  The degree cover and JOINT LIST count use the ORIGINAL 2s pair
  coefficients and component degree <K, not the parameter's degree.

  normalized linear variation. Polarization and the 2r-1 leading-
  degree product bound force the output quadratic-coefficient ratio
  to preserve a finite function space, hence be constant. Both outputs
  must use the same V, including their affine-offset differences.

- For the quadratic theorem, characteristic is NOT two. For the uniform
  Y-degree-e extension, e must be nonzero in F. The characteristic-two
  square and characteristic-three cubic families kill the corresponding
  dimension conclusions when that guard is removed.

  identity b=Psi(X,a). Its denominator depends only on X, not on Y.
  Variation gcd and primitive derivative content are removed before
  choosing two coprime derivative values. Auxiliary geometric points
  are over the algebraic closure, not assumed to exist over F.

  it is not silently applied to rational coefficients with negative degree.

  not by itself imply any polynomial pair identity; the actual raw-one

  samples or unguarded interpolation.

  space V, of actual dimension s and component degree <K.
- The graph gives an exact denominator-cleared polynomial identity for every represented
  LOW pair, not an approximate fit at sample points or an arbitrary scalar
  image count. First-coordinate projection is injective on these pairs.

  NOT used as an MCA reparameterization, so no finite label is lost at a
