# Result

PROVED: one source-fixed coordinate weighting can be funded by the
same-fiber collision discount, keeping the total resource <=(n)_(s+1).
Collision-rich cores pay additional marked-basis weight. A guarded convex
tangent makes this useful without assuming the actual core collision
count is known. Both tangent signs and whole source/degree boxes are covered.

The finite consumer still needs all-core coverage and exact original
transport. No source/core consistency exclusion, rational-atlas slope cap,
new receiver quotient or unrestricted row is assumed or proved.
