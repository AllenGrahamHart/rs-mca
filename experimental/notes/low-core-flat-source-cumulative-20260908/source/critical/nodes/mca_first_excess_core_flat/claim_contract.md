# Exact Contract

The domain consists of N>K distinct field points with nonzero evaluation
vectors, and the polynomial space has ACTUAL rank s>=3 and degree <K.
The balanced product is a rational lower threshold for ordered bases.

Deficiency implies existence of a least excess rank t<=s-2; an excess
flat does not imply deficiency. The chosen flat is complete inside the
same H, not necessarily complete or maximizing on a larger domain.
Minimality supplies caps for EVERY smaller-rank flat, not just a flag.

No received word, slope labels or owner census enters this theorem.
Falsifiers: a deficient actual polynomial source with no excess flat, or
a least-rank excess flat failing its spannedness, root or smaller-rank caps.
