# Claim contract

- A fixed smooth PROJECTIVE generic cubic identity is required, not a
  relation fitted at sampled code points or a singular rational cubic.
- Characteristic zero or >3. No p>=K condition is needed for this proof.
- Original polynomial component degrees <K, 2s affine coordinates,
  with joint agreement to the same received pair.
- Count all reduced coefficient components, retaining 3^(2s-r).
- Nonconstant j is sufficient for r=0. Constant j alone neither
  guarantees dimension one nor implies rigidity.
- For the alternative singular-fiber certificate, use the relatively
  minimal surface. Nonminimal discriminant zeros are insufficient.
- Complete own cores and raw>=1 are needed for label conversion.
  Complement of the union of cores is never substituted for an own core.
- Partition pairs with their assigned original labels. One resource and
  HIGH charge belongs to the consumer, not to each curve group.
- The ordinary factor allowance <3W applies only in the proved
  zero-dimensional branch. Two arbitrary smooth cubics do not each
  receive that allowance.
- No unrestricted relation-existence, rank, endpoint or prize closure.
