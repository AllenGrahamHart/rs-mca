# Contract

Count: ordered bases of ONE nonzero polynomial evaluation core.
The flat is complete in that core, not necessarily on the whole source.
All proper flats have the declared density cap; an original fiber cap alone
does not give the hereditary rank-profile coefficients.

The original outside fibers refine the quotient fibers. Only the former
obey the original h cap without additional hypotheses. Q bounds the latter.
The original rank-s profile is retained below the first contraction.

A collision cap must bound ordered equal-projective-fiber pairs on this
same core or a proved containing domain with the same carrier. Unrelated
receivers/carriers cannot supply it. The root-moment statement is not a
finite-slope payment. A consumer must insert actual defects, share one
tuple budget, price exceptions and prove original-source transport.
