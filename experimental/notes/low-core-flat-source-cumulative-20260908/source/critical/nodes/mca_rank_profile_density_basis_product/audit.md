# Audit

The joint capped moment (1) is a proved inherited inequality. Root capacity
must bound A/n inside that inequality, not merely use S2<=n*A: the latter
is valid but loses the useful small-rank bound.

For fixed original rank j and c=(j+1)h, (c-b)/(N-b) decreases with b
only when c<N. The proof separates c>=N, where the density expression
at K0 is >=1 and the root-density cap alone suffices. Thus no derivative
sign is silently assumed in the large-density regime.

Every actual contracted flat contains at least j independent coordinates.
The root-density ratio increases in k, so its box coefficient uses K1.
The density ratio uses K0. Both choices are uniform at every descendant.
The resulting frozen profile gives nonnegative product coefficients and
convexity on x>=1. The rank-two seed remains separate.

Six actual F17/F23 spaces check897 complete descendants, including one
with oldlambda=4, outside the previous globalguard. Actual full quotient
fibers and basis counts satisfy both point and deliberately wide degree-box
profiles. Two repeated-fiber/MDS examples retain exact equality. The old
unguarded rank-three counterexample remains valid; it does not refute this
guarded polynomial theorem.

There are270 independent unrolled-product/curvature controls and six
invalid-domain/profile controls. Normal and -O runs agree. The first control
draft accidentally supplied h=2/3 in a rank-two formula test; the domain
guard rejected that out-of-contract input, and it was corrected to h>=1.
No hand-proof statement was weakened to admit it.

Verdict: no issue at the stated scope. Exact finite controls supplement the
universal proof; independent external review and finite source transport
are separate obligations.
