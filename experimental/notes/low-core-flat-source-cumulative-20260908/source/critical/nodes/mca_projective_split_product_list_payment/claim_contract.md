# Claim contract

- The same polynomial direction space V occurs in both original factors;
  the factor direction matrix is fixed in GL_2(F). Moving F(X) directions
  do not in general preserve V or its dimension and are not covered.
- Fixed F(X)^* rescalings of the two factors can be absorbed in R if
  the remaining directions really have this constant-matrix form.
- Rational offsets are allowed. Only the both-linear-coset case uses
  transformed evaluations, and its proof first establishes regularity.
- R!=0 and m>=1. A zero product permits a zero factor and invalidates
  projectivization. No result is asserted for a parallel direction matrix.
- s is ACTUAL carrier dimension, not an upper bound inserted in a
  smaller-dimensional space without accounting. J<=A<=n is required.
- Agreement is JOINT on the original n distinct points. A divisor class,
  projective class, polynomial pair and finite slope are different objects.
- The finite consumer adds <=64 off-curve pairs and one resource/near
  base. An arbitrary curve cover, maximality of the selected endpoint,
  or transport from an original row is not supplied here.
- The general exponent m is unrelated to the normalized MCA agreement
  m=67472+J used in the consumer. Its specialization here is m=3.
