# Audit

The source-density gate is NOT assumed. Flag-free actual LOW cores get
the balanced product by the proved alternative; HIGH keeps its old weight.
Flag-bearing LOW cores retain at least one eighth of the good cost. Both
tuple constructions live on the same selected explanation and finite label;
their sizes are maximized rather than added, and different labels have
disjoint independent tuples.

The strict integer argument uses |Gamma|+near<G(J)+1+7e/8. It does not
round an intermediate expression with a fractional e coefficient. Tests
include a divisible-by-seven boundary where omitting the final +1 fails.
Uniform and pointwise mass bounds are checked independently using exact
rationals and integer products. G(J) is a comparison, not a safe row bound.

The nested flag's a counts its contracted core coordinates; dropping the
negative (10-t)*a term changes the theorem. The polynomial root bound
excludes t=8,9 on the entire interval. The t=7 quotient has dimension four
and degree <=5628, but is not claimed to preserve received-word labels
without the separate transport charges. External review remains due.
