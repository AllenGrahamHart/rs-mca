# Audit

The indicator inequality has the correct upper-bound direction for the
no-collision event. Pairs of pair events split into3*C(r,3) overlapping
and3*C(r,4) disjoint cases. The latter bracket is bounded above by T^2;
the overcount is nonnegative even at class size2.

Padding carrier zeros as singleton classes leaves T unchanged and gives
an upper resource, not a new source. MONOTONE is checked at the largest
permitted T, never inferred from samples. Small exact histograms compare
the elementary-symmetric resource to this polynomial envelope. The older
actual-source secant controls retain receiver/label ownership tests.
