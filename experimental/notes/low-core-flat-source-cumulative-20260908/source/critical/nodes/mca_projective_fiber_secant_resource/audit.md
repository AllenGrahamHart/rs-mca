# Hand Audit And Control Scope

The exceptional set depends only on the actual source, not chosen
minimizing pairs or a hypothetical child. Normalization includes h_* and
the fiber scale; omitting either gives wrong slopes. Zero evaluations
with nonzero v give one exceptional label each. Empty universal core is
essential for the other zero-evaluation case.

For a surviving label, two agreeing points in one fiber have proportional
lifted normals. They are not simply deleted as coordinates; their labels
remain unless actually in E. Every counted independent tuple is therefore
supported on distinct fibers. Full pair cores outside an old witness are
allowed because they still satisfy the same scalar equation.

The global tuple count includes unused and dependent tuples and so is an
upper bound, not an exact count of received-line witnesses. Lower costs
must come from independent tuples. The symmetric-polynomial envelope is
proved by pair averaging on a compact simplex, not inferred numerically.

verify.py uses actual F17 receiver data, a nonconstant carrier scale and
nonzero affine offset, a carrier-zero point, and examples with a same-fiber
or disjoint-fiber defect. It checks all independent triples of each source,
selected full-code-bad scalar witnesses and exact tuple ownership. Small
fiber histograms check the symmetric count and its balance envelope.
Controls supplement the hand proof; they do not enumerate the large row.
External independent mathematical review remains due.
