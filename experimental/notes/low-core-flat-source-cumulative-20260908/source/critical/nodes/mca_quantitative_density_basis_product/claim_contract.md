# Exact Contract

ACTUAL polynomial rank s>=2, distinct field points, N>K, degree<K and
nonzero evaluations are required. Density bounds ALL proper flats.
A real lambda in[1,2] satisfying floor(s^2/4)*h<=lambda*N gives the
quantitative product. No arbitrary larger loss factor is authorized.

The SAME loss factor works in every actual contraction of the original
source; this is not reapplication of an unverified density premise to
each child. Rank two uses its separate root-bound seed. Jensen and
monotonicity use real arguments>=1, not fictitious smaller-degree sources.

At lambda=1 the existing product is recovered. This does not validate
its unguarded version or imply a bad-slope census. Falsifiers: a source
satisfying these gates with too few ordered bases, or a failed hereditary
moment/rank-two/Jensen step.
