1+lambda*sum(a_i-1) on tuples using distinct fibers. All coordinates and
fibers are fixed. The stated coefficient requires integers n>r>=2;
n=r is a separately explained degenerate case, not a division by zero.

ownership of independent tuples. Padding zeros adds only singleton resource
classes with weight zero. No bad label, near event or field denominator is
dropped. One source-fixed lambda is shared by all records.

The sharpness claim concerns the full distinct-fiber resource relaxation.
The actual polynomial carrier realizes the partition, not a received line
owning every tuple. Neither MCA optimality nor a counterexample to a row
bound follows. Weighted core costs and any finite row payment are consumers,
not premises; no Bonferroni or source-collision cap is needed for SWITCH.
