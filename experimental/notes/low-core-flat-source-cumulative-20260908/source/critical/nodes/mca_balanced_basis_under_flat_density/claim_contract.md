# Claim Contract

Object: ordered independent polynomial-evaluation tuples, not bad slopes.
Domain: N=D+K distinct points over one field, D>=1, actual rank 2<=s<=K.
Every evaluation is nonzero. Proper flat sizes count coordinates, not
distinct projective points. Every proper rank-j flat has size <=j*h and
floor(s^2/4)*h<=N. These are substantive, falsifiable hypotheses.

The output is the full rational product in statement.md. Full-locator
contractions preserve D and lower actual rank. Jensen uses only a proved
polynomial, not an unproved recursive shape assumption. The MCA interface
requires the earlier same-source completed-basis/defect supplier and an
empty universal core. No field-drop, prime-subfield, maximal-raw selection,
near allowance or unconditional row safety is supplied.
