# Audit

Hand checks: the density condition applies to ALL proper flats, not only
the root fibers. A child fiber is a rank-(j+1) complete original flat minus
the already contracted a coordinates. The coefficient (s-j-1)*(j+1) is
at most floor(s^2/4); r=2 still gives n exactly as the cap. No repeated
point or projected-fiber multiplicity is omitted.

The top r-1 fibers share one degree-root budget. All their excesses above
b are nonnegative, making multiplication by the upper fiber cap valid.
Weighted Jensen is in the LOWER direction for a convex product. Its mean
argument is in the proved child range, including r=2 and K=s. The child
family is quantified over complete original flats, so induction does not
assume a newly shortened domain meets the original density condition.

The exact small controls enumerate actual flat closures and basis counts,
including a repeated-fiber equality case. Integer partitions test MOMENT.
The deliberately unqualified product fails on an actual small polynomial
space, and the existing large-fiber construction disproves it at the
finite consumer's D=67466 as well. None is an unsafe MCA construction.
Controls supplement the universal proof; external review remains due.

A second guard control uses span(1,X,P_A,X*P_A) on 0..12 over F_17,
with A=0..9 and K=12. Every root fiber is a singleton, but contracting
the point zero gives fiber sizes 9,1,1,1 and violates the balanced second
moment. A bound only on root fibers is therefore not this proof's
hereditary hypothesis. This is not asserted to refute the product itself
for that four-dimensional example.
