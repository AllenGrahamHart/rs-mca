# Audit

If the moment holds but the parent is deficient, at least one ACTUAL child
is deficient by exact contraction and Jensen. The argument stops by rank
three; rank-two moment validity is unconditional. A failed moment forces
a large child fiber by the contrapositive of the supplier's joint capped
moment, not by reversing a sufficient condition without justification.

The accumulated contraction is a complete flat F. Its size a is subtracted
both from the new flat size and from the child length, yielding the
coefficient s-t-1 in FLAG. Dropping that term or changing strictness is
incorrect. The new flat has rank t<=s-2 and root capacity K-s+t.

Controls enumerate actual complete flags and ordered bases in small
polynomial spaces. They include deficient examples, equality examples,
and a basis-rich example WITH a flag, guarding against false exclusivity.
These controls supplement the hand proof; external review remains due.
