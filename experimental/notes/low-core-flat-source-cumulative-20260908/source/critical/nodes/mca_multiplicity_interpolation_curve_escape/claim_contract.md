# Claim contract

- Object: all degree-<=w polynomial pairs with >=A complete joint
  agreements on n distinct original coordinates, lying on one irreducible
  degree-g curve over F(X).
- Hypotheses: w>=1, 1<=A<=n, r>=1, and the strict printed (ESC)
  dimension inequality. No smoothness, carrier or height assumption.
- Conclusion/unit: at most g*floor((r*A-1)/w) distinct pairs. It is
  not a bound on arbitrary slopes without the consumer's label conversion.
- Field: any characteristic. All jets are Hasse coefficients. Only the
  geometric intersection degree is evaluated after field extension.
- Completeness: all rich pairs satisfy every full multiplicity relation;
  one relation escapes the curve factor and counts them all by Bezout.
- Nonclaims: a curve cover without the strict dimension test, arbitrary numerical
  rows, a general safe endpoint, LIST closure or either prize.
- Falsifier: a source satisfying (ESC) with more than the printed number
  of rich pairs on G, or failure of the Gauss/root/intersection steps.
- The full-kernel corollary separately supplies a degree-<g0 gcd cover
  under (ESC) with g replaced by g0; off-gcd pairs and the enlarged
  factor height are explicit. A cover alone is not an MCA source payment.
