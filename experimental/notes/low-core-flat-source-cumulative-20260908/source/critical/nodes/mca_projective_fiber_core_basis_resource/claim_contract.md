  original field, distinct evaluation coordinates and distinct finite slopes.

  evaluations. Its cardinality a is neither a parameter-class count nor a
  field-degree factor. Common V-zeros are not included in a.
- Raw is minimized in the SAME fixed V on the chosen support. No canonical
  maximization, near assumption or pair-curve cover is needed.
- Locator division is used solely to count core evaluation bases. It does
  not replace the original source by an unproved quotient receiver.
- Two disjoint inside-count classes of core bases may be added. New and
  old counts of the SAME completed incidence tuples must combine by maximum.
- Any finite source payment and original near allowance are separate from
  this generic count. No arbitrary-carrier classification or prize closure.

actual core whose independent evaluation-basis count is below (1).

of the remaining degree excess e. External independent review is due.
