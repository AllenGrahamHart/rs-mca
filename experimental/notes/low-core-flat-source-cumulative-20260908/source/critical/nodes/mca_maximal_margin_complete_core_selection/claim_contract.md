# Claim contract

- Maximize over all exact bad supports AND all explanations in one fixed
  carrier. Labels, field, receiver, degree and carrier remain unchanged.
- 1<=T and 2T<d; the strict boundary is essential.
- Full-code-contained neighboring supports are included in the exchange
  argument; their containing second polynomial need not initially lie in V.
- Complete defect refers to the selected scalar explanation's entire
  agreement set. It does not describe the source outside that set.
- Rank-one exclusion concerns finite collision slopes (beta!=0), and
  component-polynomial rank over F, not pointwise rank over a subfield.
- Error rank is retested after maximization. Finite selection existence
  is a proof device, not an instruction to enumerate the deployed field.
- Nonclaims: absence of zero/cyclic spaces, payment of rank-two cross
  incidences, unchanged error rank under arbitrary reselection, either prize.
