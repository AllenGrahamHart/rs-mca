# Dimension result

PROVED LOCAL: bounded polynomial pairs on a smooth generic cubic have
coefficient dimension <=1; nonconstant j gives dimension zero.

The proof retains singular specialized fibers, treats constant products
separately, and differentiates only after spreading an actual family.
It extends the recognized geometric classes available to the critical
algebraic-counting route. It does not prove arbitrary cubic coverage or
either prize. No upstream acceptance or formal certification is claimed.
