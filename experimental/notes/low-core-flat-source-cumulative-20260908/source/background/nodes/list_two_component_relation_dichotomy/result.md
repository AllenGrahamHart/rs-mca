M_2(a)<=M(a)+(M(a)-1)M(2a-k+1), with both scalar inputs on the
same field, domain and code dimension. This covers the relation-free
class without a new joint hypothesis. The scalar upper inputs are
not proved here; no prize count or endpoint is claimed.

PROVED: the worst two-component common-support RS list size equals
the maximum of the ordinary scalar worst-case size and the worst size
among received pairs with no bounded-degree relation.

A nonzero relation gives an exact scalar correspondence after the
necessary gcd-root treatment. Two independent relations give at most
one codeword pair. The dimension gate removes the relation-free class
when 3a>n+2k-2. Otherwise its genuine codeword count remains open.

The proof covers arbitrary received words over the declared field.
It supplies a source-bound exhaustive reduction, not an upper atom,
new field descent, larger prime-field floor or prize endpoint.
