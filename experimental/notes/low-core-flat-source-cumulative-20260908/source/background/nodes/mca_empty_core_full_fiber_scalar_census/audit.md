# Audit

The hand proof checks scalar-family membership, exceptional labels,
complete-set lifting, actual child carrier and zero coordinates separately.
It then counts support incidences, not independently budgeted slope sets.
The profile argument retains the joint top-(s-1) fiber constraint and both
endpoint profiles. Its continuous relaxation never changes a source degree.

The key new scope is that a scalar label may lie in a receiver-color
family different from its minimizing pair's class. Thus the previous
disjoint pair-group count cannot simply be relabelled as this incidence
ledger. The proof of the generalized transport is included explicitly.

Small-field controls exercise that distinction, essential exceptions,
complete versus selected agreement sets, nonconstant scales and a retained
carrier-zero incidence. Exact partition controls test both profiles for
a known decreasing convex rational F. These controls supplement, not
certify, the universal proof or realizability of relaxed partitions. Both normal
and -O replays pass: 62 surviving color/fiber children, two actual sources,
one retained zero incidence, 476 exact partitions and 59 wrong-minimum
controls. Final normal/-O runs, including the explicit raw-one checks,
take 0.06/0.13 seconds and 14464/17060 KiB RSS.

The first replay exposed an overstrict fixture expectation: the first
source witnesses a child becoming good if exceptions are not removed;
the scaled second source instead tests a retained carrier-zero incidence
and need not have a good exceptional child. The universal bad-survivor
checks were unchanged. The fixture now requires the essential-exception
counterexample in the first source and the zero-incidence check in the second.

An exploratory iteration of the profile expression remains over budget
at the inspected residual degrees. Its recursively required shape gates
were NOT proved; it is not an MCA certificate or a method-impossibility
theorem. No finite interval or new unsafe source is inferred from that probe.
