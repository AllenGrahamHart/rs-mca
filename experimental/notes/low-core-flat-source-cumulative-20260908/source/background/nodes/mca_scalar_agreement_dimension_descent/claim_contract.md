# Claim contract

- One fixed received pair, one affine explanation space of actual dimension
  s over the ambient field F, and one selected exact bad support per
  DISTINCT finite slope. A merely coefficient-subfield-linear space is
  not substituted: the matching gauge uses arbitrary scalars in F.
- At a nonzero evaluation, choose a common polynomial pair in
  (h_*+C') times C' matching the received pair. This is not a minimizing
  pair and need not correspond to any original core.
- The child retains the original slope and the original support minus the
  anchor. Its containing polynomial code has dimension K-1.
- Common evaluation zeros contribute g|Z|+tau, not automatically g|Z|.
  The one-record exceptional charge uses distinct slope labels.
- A uniform child cap means every resulting received pair and every
  smaller actual explanation dimension, not just one observed child.
- The two-anchor companion requires 3<=s<=K and no common evaluation
  zero on the current domain. Its fibers are projective evaluations on
  C', not the lifted MCA normals involving the received direction. Both
  envelope branches and w_i+w_j<=K-s+2 must be retained. Its U bounds
  all actual second children in dimension at most s-2.
- A consumer with common zeros must normalize them and pay exceptional
  slopes before invoking that companion. A complete shared agreement core
  is not the intersection of arbitrarily selected size-m supports.
- Rank zero is an exact SELECTED-family result, not the full MCA set of
  the constructed line. No post-near preservation is claimed.
- The concrete consumer still requires a complete post-near selection
  with the stated error-rank bound. Arbitrary-source coverage is open.
