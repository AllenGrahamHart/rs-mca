# Claim contract

- Object: local degree-bounded polynomial evaluation sections on the union
  of actual joint cores of one received pair, and the span of that pair's
  two classes modulo global polynomial sections.
- Field/domain: arbitrary field and finite distinct evaluation points;
  no smoothness or prime-field normalization is assumed.
- General theorem: supported-dual identity, received-pair defect trichotomy,
  and sufficient sequential-overlap criterion in statement.md and proof.md.
- High-band theorem: the explicit all-projective ceiling A=k+h, h>=2;
  distinct degree-<k pairs with complete cores of size k+d, 1<=d<=h-1.
  The depth-sum threshold is d_i+d_j>=h.
- Counted quantities: pairs are distinct polynomial pairs; slopes use the
  existing exact-A first-match support selector. A single pair at depth d
  uses h-d outside points per slope, giving the printed cap.
- Projection/normalization: no change of received line, support, slope,
  code degree, radius or field denominator. No probability conversion.
- Dependencies: self-contained finite-dimensional duality, interpolation
  and the root bound; no numerical or unproved input.
- Nonclaims: no defect-two population bound, general LIST/MCA payment,
  equivalence of gluing and window ranks, or closure of either prize.

The high-band proof gives a route boundary, not a new conjectural child:
defect two is equivalent to having at least two high-band pairs, so merely
renaming that population is not a smaller critical obligation.
