# Claim contract

The domain may be arbitrary, but the field is fixed and must have enough
unused points for padding. The uniform statement assumes |F|>=2R.
This gate is satisfied on the actual KoalaBear field and all unchanged-field
children. It must not be silently removed when using a smaller field.

The full Johnson theorem at the padded degree applies to arbitrary domains;
the added points need not preserve a multiplicative subgroup. Padding
preserves each original finite slope, full-code pair noncontainment and
the selected affine ranks. It need not preserve all extra padded slopes.

Removing a common zero is NOT asserted lossless. A nonuniversal zero can
agree at one exceptional slope, whose label is discarded. The allowance
R-L explicitly pays a chain of these removals. Children can have higher
agreement and lower actual explanation dimension; both cases are retained.
No inherited post-near or raw-low margin is used.

The seven Johnson gates and all integer ceilings are explicit. Their
inequalities are sufficient certificates, not claims of exact MCA maxima.
The stronger constants do not prove V_11 affordable or rank twelve closed.
