labels; one global resource; and separate preferred-direction charges.

strict remainder degree <m-T; every low pair has enough roots. A low
pair proves gcd divisibility, and coverage by low cores proves primitive
source compatibility even at those gcd roots.

component spaces. It remains true over finite fields by polynomial
divisibility/degree, with no assumption of an infinite field.

they are explicitly removed at cost <=n. Outside-coordinate ownership
is used only for complete cores of represented low pairs. High labels
stay in the original weighted resource. The finite consumer separately
checks all numerical inputs and actual small-field controls.
