# Contract

Object: complete evaluation flats and polynomial annihilators in ONE fixed
M-point core H. Actual carrier dimension11, degree<K, M>K, no zero evaluations.
This is not the complete domain of size1048576+J or a family of received lines.

The maximizing-rank-six theorem requires K>=26 and the ALL-proper-flat
maximum h>(K-4)/7. Only rank-six flats attaining exactly that maximum are
counted. All have the same a=6h. The packing clause additionally requires
36h>M. A lower bound on one flat's density is not this maximum-density gate.
The separate near-maximum version requires an integer b<=6h with
2b-5h>K-4, and its OWN positive denominator b^2-Mh. Its count is not
automatically25 or17. The same whole-H maximum h controls intersections.

The more general three-flat lemma retains pairwise full joins and the
strict outside-root inequality. Without that inequality the rational
conclusion can fail, as the F23 control shows.

Falsifiers: a pair of maximizing six-flats with a proper join under the gate;
a family larger than PACK with36h>M; or a nonzero cross determinant despite
the strict outside-root bound. A tuple whose hypotheses fail is not a falsifier.

Outputs: flat count, same-two-anchor rational directions, and polynomial
degree/divisibility constraints. No count of selected slopes, minimizing
pairs or all possible core/anchor choices is claimed. Original higher ranks,
rank1..5 maximum flats and flats outside the explicit band are not priced.
