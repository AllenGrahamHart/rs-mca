the ordered r-tuples using distinct fibers. Give coordinate x weight

all positions of all tuples in G_r. Then

```text
|G_r| + ((r-1)/(2(n-r))) W_r <= (n)_r.                 (SWITCH)
```

The [proof](source/critical/nodes/mca_projective_fiber_switching_resource/proof.md)
is a short double count. From a good tuple, mark position i, choose another
coordinate in its fiber, and replace any position j!=i by that coordinate.

fiber. Its incoming multiplicity is exactly 2(n-|S_b|), where S_b is the
union of all represented fibers, hence at most 2(n-r). Such tuples and
the good tuples are disjoint parts of the full resource. This proves SWITCH.
